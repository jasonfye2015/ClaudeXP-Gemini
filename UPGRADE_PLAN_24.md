# Upgrade plan 24: monsters, heroes and people (v29.0 - v29.5)

Asked for 2026-11-01: items 8-13 of the list ("2. Monsters, heroes and people"). The sprite packs have no frames to
animate (checked before: the paper-doll parts, about 975, are the only pieces), so the motion is made in code, from
the pictures the game has - no new downloads.

## v29.0 - monsters that move (8)
- The picture drawn in strips that bend: a sway at rest, a bob and stepping feet on the move, the wind-up, the strike
  and the recoil in a fight.

## v29.1 - which way it faces (9)
- A monster has a facing (the way it last moved, or its own); seen from the side it narrows and darkens on the far
  edge, from behind it shows its back - its picture darkened, blurred and turned.

## v29.2 - deaths of their own (10)
- The bones crumble, the burning and the demons go to ash, the crystal ones shatter, the drowned and anything dying in
  water sinks with a splash, the ghosts rise (as they did).

## v29.3 - the heroes' gear seen (11)
- The portraits on the party's cards, the sheets and the level-up card show the weapon in hand, the helm and the
  armour's colour.

## v29.4 - townsfolk with routines (12)
- The six residents and a few townsfolk walk the town by the clock - home, the shop or the forge, the market and the
  well, the tavern in the evening - and stand under the eaves when it rains.

## v29.5 - followers seen (13)
- The porter, the torchbearer (and the torch's light), the hired dog, the party's own hound and pony walk behind the
  party, where a look back finds them.
