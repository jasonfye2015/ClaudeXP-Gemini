# Upgrade plan 26: the view and the screen (v31.0 - v31.9)

Asked for 2026-11-02: all of set 10 of the new list (items 49-58). The picture's only. Several phases share one new
screen pass (the glow's, made general in v31.2): the frame is drawn off screen when any of them needs it.

## v31.0 - a hand and a torch (49)
- The front hero's hand and torch at the foot of the view: it sways with the walk, the flame gutters in the wind and
  burns low as the torch does, and it is lowered (shaded) when the torch is shaded. Not by day above ground.

## v31.1 - camera feel (50)
- A head-bob and a little sway in the walk, a lean into turns, a stumble when a hero is hit hard, a shake when something
  huge arrives. Settings > Camera motion: full, gentle, off (off with Reduce motion too).

## v31.2 - living portraits (51)
- The heroes on the party's cards blink and glance; bruised and pale when hurt, sweaty when the party is tired,
  frosted in the snow.

## v31.3 - focus (52)
- The view behind a dialog goes soft; a boss's entrance pulls the focus onto it.

## v31.4 - spell colours in the room (53)
- A bolt in flight lights the walls in its colour (fire orange, frost blue, poison green...), and lightning flashes the
  whole room.

## v31.5 - screen styles (54)
- Settings > Screen style: clean (as now), old monitor (scanlines, a curve, colour fringes), watercolour (soft washes,
  darker edges, paper), ink and parchment (ink lines on old paper), crisp (sharpened).

## v31.6 - scene transitions (55)
- The shaft: to black and back with the descent; a portal: a swirl; the dawn after the camp brightens slowly from a
  warm dark; a new floor fades in.

## v31.7 - 3-D items (56)
- Items in the pack and the shops shown as small solid things that turn (the icon given depth, layer on layer) when
  looked at; the item card's picture turns slowly.

## v31.8 - the map as an object (57)
- The big map on parchment: ink lines with a hand's wobble, new squares inked in with a spreading blot, drawn icons,
  and the party's route in pencil.

## v31.9 - weather on the lens (58)
- Raindrops running down the view in the rain, snow settling at its edges, beads of mist in the deep and in fog.
  Settings > Weather on the view: on / off.
