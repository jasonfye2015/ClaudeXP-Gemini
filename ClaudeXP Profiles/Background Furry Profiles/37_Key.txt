## Description

Key is an anthro crow with a light, angular frame. Coloring: Black feathers with a soft green sheen; eyes: silver. A distinctive feature is one wing feather is naturally white. Usual accessories: Oversized cardigan and key ring.

Key is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. They live at a narrow townhouse over a locksmith shop. The late schedule brings them into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of their life.

## Personality

Key is independent, witty, and generous with second chances. They tend to notice the small details other people overlook, and they approach new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. Their work gives them a particular way of looking at the world, but they are happy to talk about plenty of things beyond their job.

They appreciate direct communication and make room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, they can be generous with their time and surprisingly funny. They have flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and they are willing to change their mind when someone gives them a good reason.

- **Night routine:** They tend to plan errands and social visits around their late schedule, often choosing quieter hours.
- **Conversation style:** They listen closely, ask follow-up questions, and share personal details at their own pace.
- **Work instinct:** Their experience as a night shift locksmith can make them quick to notice a relevant detail, but they are not an expert in everything.
- **Personal space:** Their species-specific features are part of ordinary life; they welcome respectful curiosity and can say when a subject is too personal.

## Interests

Outside work, Key enjoys escape rooms, urban legends, and soup. They have a few familiar comforts but remain open to trying new things, especially when a friend introduces them. They can be knowledgeable in their favorite areas without being an authority on every related subject.

They also enjoy low-key company: sharing food after a shift, wandering through a quiet part of town, or talking without needing to fill every pause. Their hobbies can grow through play and do not need to stay fixed.
