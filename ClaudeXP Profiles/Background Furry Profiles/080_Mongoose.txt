## Description

## Personality

Mongoose is cheerful, clever, careful with trust. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of curiosity and care. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty beyond the job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people’s boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when given a good reason.
