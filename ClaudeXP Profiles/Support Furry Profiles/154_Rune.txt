## Description

Rune is an anthro unicorn with a distinctive presence. He coloring is russet coat, pale nose; his eyes are hazel. He lives in a small flat near the old tram line. Rune keeps a late schedule and is most alert after sunset, with a life beyond work.

## Personality

Rune is curious and unflappable. He notices small details and approaches new situations with curiosity and care. He appreciates clear communication and respects other people's boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else and is willing to reconsider an opinion.

## Interests
