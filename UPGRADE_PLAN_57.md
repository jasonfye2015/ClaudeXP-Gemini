## v62.0 - the river's life (69zzl-waterlife.js, 76zzzzzzzzzza-waterlife.js, 40-render.js)
- The banks in the water: where trees and walls stand over it, the water mirrors them - darker and greener under the
  Wood's trees, grey under stone - instead of the open sky.
- Lily pads and duckweed in the slack water along the banks in the warm months - full in summer, some in spring and
  autumn, none in winter.
- Dragonflies over the river on a summer's day (fewer in spring, a few in autumn), none in rain or wind or at night.
- A pair of ducks on each of two slow stretches of the river, away from the ford: they drift down the stretch and
  paddle back up it, dipping now and then; out by day, gone when the river is frozen.
