Claudenites 3-D - the art, and where it came from. All of it is Claude and Magic's, brought across by
tools/import_cm.py; every source is CC0 (it asks nothing; the credit is given anyway) - save the farm's crops and
produce, which are CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 and credited in full below.

The figures (assets/figures/figures.bin): rigged, animated low-poly bodies, as Claude and Magic bakes them.
- KayKit: Adventurers Character Pack and Skeletons Character Pack - Kay Lousberg, www.kaylousberg.com (CC0):
  https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers, https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-skeletons
- Quaternius (quaternius.com, CC0): Ultimate Animated Animal Pack, Easy Enemy Pack, Ultimate Monsters,
  Animated Dinosaur Pack, Animated Fish Pack, Ultimate Platformer Pack.

The plants and props (assets/figures/plants.bin): static vertex-coloured meshes.
- Quaternius - Ultimate Nature Pack (CC0): https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html
- Kenney - Pirate Kit (CC0; the barrels, the crates and the chest - assets/figures/chest.npz, by tools/bake_chest.py):
  https://kenney.nl/assets/pirate-kit

The textures (assets/textures): ambientCG (CC0), https://ambientcg.com - Bricks089, PavingStones141, Rock035, Rock037,
Rock020, Bricks058, Bricks100, PavingStones128, Planks039, Marble006, Tiles074, Onyx013, Grass001, Grass005,
Ground110, Ground103, Plaster001, RoofingTiles013A, Bark007, Bricks097, Ground077, ScatteredLeaves001; cut-outs
Foliage001, Foliage006, LeafSet027, LeafSet024.

The painted art and the set pieces (assets/cm): Claude and Magic's own, as it draws them in code (its walls, dressings,
furniture, landmarks and set-piece things) and writes them (its sixty set-piece rooms and their lines), exported from
the running game by tools/export_cm_art.py.

The ground's blending (engine/groundmap.py, engine/render.py's WORLD_FS): Claude and Magic's own, carried over from its
shader and its ground's wear and forest floor.

The music: ClaudeRPG's own - its sound engine (SND) and its worlds' music styles (MSTY), by Kithylin, MIT licence
(ClaudeGames/clauderpg/game/LICENSE) - carried over into Python in engine/music.py, note for note.

The item pictures (assets/items, by tools/import_icons.py), both CC0:
- Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup's tiles (the Crawl Stone Soup team and the RLTiles project) - the robes, hats, gloves,
  boots, rings, amulets, wands and gold piles: https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles
- Henrique Lazarini (7Soul1), "496 pixel art icons for medieval/fantasy RPG" - the gems and runes of the wand
  parts, the potions, the scroll and the coin: https://opengameart.org/content/496-pixel-art-icons-for-medievalfantasy-rpg
  (kept in Local Storage/Claudenites icons/7soul-496-rpg-icons).

The farm's pictures (assets/items/crop_*.png, veg_*.png, by tools/import_icons.py) - CC-BY-SA 3.0+ or GPL 3.0+, and so
these pictures stay under that licence wherever they go:
- "[LPC] Crops" by bluecarrot16, Daniel Eddeland (daneeklu), Joshua Taylor, Richard Kettering (Jetrel), commissioned by
  castelonia: https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-crops - the growing crops, through Claude and Magic's crop sheet
  (its src/art/crops.png). Its attribution statement and licence table, whole: assets/items/CREDITS-crops.txt.
  The sown-earth picture is josehzz's "Farming Crops 16x16" (CC0): https://opengameart.org/content/farming-crops-16x16
- "Fruit and Veggie Inventory" by Joshua Taylor (CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0), part of the Liberated Pixel Cup:
  https://opengameart.org/content/fruit-and-veggie-inventory - the harvested produce (and the meals made of one crop).
The food (meat, fish, mushroom, the dishes) and the seed pouch are 7Soul1's and Crawl's, CC0, as above; so are the
Deep's empty bottles and loose bones (7Soul1's I_Bottle01-04, E_Bones02-03, I_Bone).

The sounds are made by the game as it starts (engine/sound.py); the item pictures fall back to painted ones (ui/icons.py).
