# Upgrade plan 30: followers and pets, deeper (v35.0 - v35.4)

Asked for 2026-11-02: set 2 of the second list (items 6-10); the hound's tracking leads to a lair - a cave, or more
than one, in the Wood where the fled beast went to ground. Followers (64b-people.js) and the dog and pony (69x-pets.js)
stand already.

## v35.0 - followers who level (6)
- A follower who comes through five new floors is seasoned, through ten a veteran: tougher, and better at their trade -
  the porter carries more, the torchbearer keeps the flame in a gust and then spots traps, the hedge-wizard mends twice
  a floor and lights longer, the farm's dog goes deeper.

## v35.1 - the hound's tricks (7)
- Tricks taught at the farm: fetch (brings back what the fled dropped), guard (no surprise at a camp's rest, and the
  party sleeps better), track (follows a beast that fled to its lair: a den in the Deep Wood, a cave of its own, with the
  beast and its kin and what they keep).

## v35.2 - the pony's gear (8)
- Saddlebags (more carried), a blanket (the cold and snow don't slow it), and a cart (the fields' produce hauled to
  market from the silo, at a better price).

## v35.3 - a follower's fear (9)
- On the dark floors and the deep bands a follower may lose their nerve and bolt for the stair if the party's heart is
  low - the more seasoned, the steadier.

## v35.4 - the kennel and the stable (10)
- In town, by the Keep-yard: a kennel and a stable where the animals live when the party is not out - seen through the
  gate - and where they are fed, named again, and taught.
