# Upgrade plan 19: the world (v24.0 - v24.6)

Asked for 2026-11-01: items 20-26 of the graphics list - "4. The world". One release each, in the order that lets the
later ones lean on the earlier (the wind moves the water and the leaves; the seasons and the hours feed the life).

## v24.0 - wind (22)
- On the surface the wind blows from a way of its own each day, in gusts that roll across the land: trees, grass,
  reeds, flowers and crops give to it together, the scarecrow's coat flaps. Rain brings it up, mist lays it down.

## v24.1 - living water (21)
- Rings spread where the party walks in water and where rain falls on puddles and the river; the river runs (its
  ripples carried downstream with the current); still pools mirror the sky and what stands about them more clearly.

## v24.2 - seasons that turn (23)
- The season comes in over days, not overnight: in the last days of summer the crowns colour a few trees at a time,
  through autumn the leaves fall and gather on the ground and the crowns thin; snow builds up over days of snowfall and
  lies patchy at first, and melts back the same way.

## v24.3 - day and night life (26)
- Lit windows go dark one by one at bedtime and come back before dawn; a lamplighter walks the streets at dusk lighting
  the lamps; moths about the lamps, owls calling and passing in the Wood, bats at dusk, fireflies on summer nights.

## v24.4 - distant views (25)
- Beyond the edge of each place, what lies beyond it: from the town the Wood's treeline and the Mage Tower on the
  skyline, from the Wood the town's walls and roofs, its windows lit at night.

## v24.5 - real 3-D props (20)
- Barrels, crates, carts, market stalls, fences and benches built as small solid models, lit and shadowed like the
  walls, instead of flat pictures.

## v24.6 - interiors (24)
- The shops, the tavern, the library and the temple can be walked into: a furnished room of their own (counter,
  shelves, tables, hearth, the keeper behind the counter), and the dialogs open from inside.
