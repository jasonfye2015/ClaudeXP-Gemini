# UPGRADE_PLAN_75 - Choices with costs

From the gameplay systems review (asked for: an RPG systems designer's evaluation - deeper, more interconnected play;
recommendations with what each does, the experience, the interactions, the dependencies and the risks; then built
incrementally once approved). The review's three challenges: breadth has outrun connection (many systems feed only
themselves), small passive bonuses have become invisible ("bonus soup"), and going home costs too little. So these
favour choices with costs and connections between what exists, and add little new machinery.

## Order (approved: start with the suggested three)
1. **Vows.** At Claude's desk, up to three vows a run, each to the end of the next band: Dry Throat (no healing
   draughts), No Retreat (no stair back up, no Return to Claude), Unbought (nothing bought), Four Together (no hero
   falls), Unrested (no camp rest). Kept to the floor: a ring named for the vow with a fitting gift. Broken: a bad omen
   (the party's heart sinks) and a line in Claude's account. Asked once before a choice breaks one; the Automatic hand
   never breaks one; red ink voids them. Connections: Claude's account and narration, red ink, the potions' one
   funnel (give), the shops (buy), the camps (rest), the stairs and Return to Claude.
2. **Your own dead come back.** Heroes on the memorial may rise in the band where they fell, on later runs, as named
   revenants with the gear they fell with; laid to rest, their gear comes home, the memorial wall marks them at rest,
   and Claude writes an epilogue. One a band at most, scaled to the floor.
3. **The king's bargains.** At a few milestones, offers from the Lich King - each a real gain with a real cost, shown
   honestly before it's taken - that the throne remembers.

Later, if wanted: a village that reacts (prices that move with what's brought home), light and shadow as tactics,
factions of the Deep, the tension of retreat, tickets to the deep, and the experimental "write it differently".

Each release: tests of the feature and of the systems it touches (including whole runs played by the Automatic hand),
harness checks, and the usual ship.
