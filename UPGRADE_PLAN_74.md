# UPGRADE_PLAN_74 - The account: presentation

From the visual development review (asked for: an art director's and UI/UX designer's pass over the whole game,
recommendations ranked by impact and complexity, then built in the suggested order once approved).

## The art direction
"The account": Claude is writing the story, so what he writes - the journal, the map, the chapter cards, the item
cards, his notes - is parchment and ink, in his hand; the world itself stays stone, torchlight and weather. Every
change below follows that one rule, so the game reads as one thing.

## Order (as approved: 2, 3, 4; then 5, 6; then 7, 9; then 10)
1. **The view, decluttered** (review 2): the sky card compact by default (dial and hour; the date, weather and moon on
   hover, or Full in Settings), out of the way in a fight; the vignette deeper below ground.
   **Health and status at a glance** (review 3): health bars green, amber, then red and pulsing when low; ailments as
   coloured chips with their mark; a badly hurt hero's portrait dims; the acting hero's frame lit in a fight.
   **Chapter cards** (review 4): the floor and its band across the view in Claude's hand as you go down; a band's
   first floor is a chapter, with his line. Settings: On or Off; never under Automatic.
2. **Light with depth** (review 5) - mostly there already (cast shadows from sun, lamps and the torch, swaying flame
   shadows, soft corners along the walls, lamp light stopped by walls): so a targeted pass - a deeper torch pool below
   ground (Settings: Lighting, Deep or Classic) and firmer shadows under feet.
   **Combat that hits** (review 6): crits in Claude's red ink, a thin edge flash coloured by the element of a spell's
   hit, a short hit-stop on a crit, and a foe's last moment as a stroke of ink.
3. **The pack and the gear** (review 7): the inventory as two panes - the hero and their slots, and the pack as a grid
   of framed items - with the item as a parchment card; the character sheet's hierarchy cleared.
   **Claude's map** (review 9): the automap drawn as his - ink lines, hatching for the unknown, the rooms' names.
4. **Enemy silhouettes** (review 10): each family its own shape from a small kit, so a kobold never reads as a person.

Each release: the existing systems kept, targeted changes only; tests, harness shots, the usual ship.
