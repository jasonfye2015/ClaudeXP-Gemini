# Upgrade plan 59: the real-time places (v64.0)

Asked for 2026-11-05: the last of six sets. The set was first suggested as "interiors" - torches with real light,
drips and cobwebs in the cave, banners and arrow slits in the Fortress - but the dungeon's interiors already had
their moving shadows, trim, worn floors, ceilings, low fog and daylight through the grates (plans 35, 36, 43, 44),
and the cave, the Fortress and Claudebros are drawn on their own canvases, so this plan is their look.

## v64.0 - the places' look (64zzze-placelook.js, 76zzzzzzzzzzc-places.js)
- The Fortress: the torch's light breathes and warms what is near it, dust drifts in it, and the view's corners fall
  into the dark.
- Claudeita's cave: water drips from the roof about the hero and splashes where it lands; the deep has a cold blue
  glow at its edge, deeper down.
- Claudebros: the hour lies over the sky - warm at dusk and dawn, a deep blue with stars at night, by the game's own
  clock - and clouds cross every kind of level, not only the meadow's.
