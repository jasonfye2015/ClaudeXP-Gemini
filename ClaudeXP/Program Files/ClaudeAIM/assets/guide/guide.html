ClaudeAIM Guide

Welcome back to 2001! ClaudeAIM is an instant messenger in the style of the classic AOL Instant Messenger, except every buddy on your list is a character powered by an AI model running on your own computer (through Ollama). Your buddies have their own personalities, lives, time zones and sleep schedules. They sign on and off, go away, remember your conversations, send you pictures, and message you first.

Use the list on the left to jump to a section.

 Quick Start

  1. Start Ollama, then start ClaudeAIM (ClaudeAIM.bat).
  2. Listen to the modem, then type a Screen Name and click Sign On.
  3. Your Buddy List appears. Buddies in black are online.
  4. Click a buddy to open an Instant Message window, type, and press Enter.
  5. Look for the 📝 next to a name: that buddy has a message waiting for you.

 Signing On

Dialing

Every session starts the old-fashioned way, with the modem dialing in. The status goes from Dialing... to Connecting... to Connected....

  • Cancel quits ClaudeAIM.
  • Check Skip this during log on to go straight to the Sign On window next time. (You can turn it back on in Setup.)

The Sign On window

  • Screen Name: what your buddies will call you. You choose it the first time you sign on, and after that it's locked in. The only way to change it is Reset All in Setup.
  • Password: just for the feel of it. ClaudeAIM runs entirely on your computer, so there's nothing to log in to and the password isn't checked.
  • Save password: remembers what you typed in the password box.
  • Auto-login: skips this window next time and signs you straight on.
  • Help opens this Guide. Setup opens Setup.
  • Sign On (or press Enter) connects you.

Connecting

After a moment you'll hear the famous "Welcome!" and see Connected!, then your Buddy List opens. Cancel takes you back to the Sign On window.

 The Buddy List

The Buddy List is home base. Its title shows your screen name, and changes to *Away* while you're away.

Online and offline

  • Buddies lists who's online, with a count like (5/12). Names are in black.
  • Offline lists everyone else, in grey italics.
  • When you sign on, about half of your list is already online (whoever is awake at that hour). The rest sign on over time.
  • Click the small box next to a group name to fold or unfold it.

What the little pictures mean

IconMeaning
🚪The buddy just signed on or off. You'll hear a door sound, and the door stays for 60 seconds.
📝Message waiting! The buddy sent you something you haven't seen yet. It goes away once you open their window and look at it.
❗The buddy is away from their computer.

If messages arrived while ClaudeAIM was closed, they're still waiting next time, and you'll hear "You've got mail!" just after you sign on.

Opening a conversation

Click any buddy's name to open an Instant Message window with them. This works for offline buddies too: you can read your old conversation and leave them a message.

Menus

File

  • Set Away Status...: put up an away message.
  • Set Buddy Icon...: choose your picture.
  • Setup...: models and options (Setup).
  • Exit: signs off with the classic "Goodbye!". (Closing the Buddy List window doesn't sign you off; it tucks ClaudeAIM into the system tray.)

 The System Tray

Once you're signed on, the little running man lives in the system tray (the icons by the clock on your taskbar). He only appears after you sign on, not during dial-up or on the Sign On window.

Can't see the tray icon?

Windows 11 hides new tray icons by default and tucks them away behind the ^ arrow next to the clock. To find him, click the ^ arrow; the running man will be in the little pop-up.

To keep him on your taskbar all the time, do one of these:

  • Drag the running man out of the ^ pop-up and drop him on the taskbar next to the other icons.
  • Or open Settings > Personalization > Taskbar > Other system tray icons and switch Python on.

 Windows lists ClaudeAIM as Python, because that's the program running it. If you start ClaudeAIM in two different ways (for example from Thonny and from ClaudeAIM.bat), you may see two Python entries in that list. Switch on both. 

Using the tray icon

  • Message waiting: the icon flashes between the running man and the running man with a 📝 until you've read everything. Hover over it to see who's waiting.
  • Click the icon to open the waiting message, or your Buddy List if nothing is waiting.
  • Right-click it for Open Buddy List, Set Away Status... (or I'm Back while you're away) and Exit.
  • Closing the Buddy List window keeps ClaudeAIM running in the tray, so your buddies can still reach you. To sign off completely, use Exit (in the tray menu or the Buddy List's File menu).

 Instant Messages

Each buddy gets their own Instant Message window. Drag its edges or corners to resize it: the conversation grows to fill the space. New IM windows open at the size you last used.

Reading

  • The top box is your conversation. Your buddy's name is red, yours is blue. Scroll up to see everything you've ever said to each other. Your whole history is saved.
  • Every message has a date and time. The date is today, 25 years ago: it's always 2001 in ClaudeAIM.
  • Your buddy's messages show their local time. A buddy in England will be several hours ahead of you (in the picture above, Xavier's 4:01 PM is your 11:01 AM).
  • Your buddy's icon is on the left of the conversation, and yours is on the left of the typing box.

Writing

  • Type in the bottom box and press Enter or click Send.
  • Shift+Enter starts a new line without sending.
  • Send a few messages in a row if you like: your buddy waits until you've finished and answers them all together.
  • Closing the window clears anything you'd typed but not sent. The conversation itself is kept.

The status bar

The strip along the bottom shows what your buddy is doing:

  • is typing...: a reply is on its way.
  • is downloading image...: they're receiving a picture you sent.
  • is away. or is offline.

Get Info

Get Info (bottom left) shows the buddy's profile, and their local time if they live in a different time zone.

The memory meter

The little bar under Send shows how much of your buddy's memory the conversation is using. It fills from green through yellow to red. When it's full, your buddy starts forgetting the oldest messages to make room for new ones. They're still in your saved log; your buddy just can't recall them any more. A bigger context size in Setup gives buddies a longer memory, but uses more of your computer's memory.

Away and offline buddies

  • Message a buddy who's away and you'll get their away message as an Auto response. They'll answer properly when they come back.
  • While you are away, buddies who message you get your away message as an auto response, and you'll see it in the window too.
  • For offline buddies, see Messaging offline buddies.

When you're not looking

If a message arrives while a buddy's window is minimized or behind other windows, the window flashes on the taskbar, the tray icon flashes, and the buddy gets a 📝 on your Buddy List until you bring the window back to the front.

Menus

File

  • Gallery...: every picture you and this buddy have shared (Gallery).
  • Close: closes the window.

 Messaging Offline Buddies

Just like real AIM, you can message a buddy who isn't online. You'll see a grey note saying your messages will be delivered when they sign on. Leave as many messages (and pictures) as you like.

They're kept even if you close ClaudeAIM. The next time that buddy signs on, they read everything you left and reply after a minute or so. You'll get the 📝 and the "message received" sound like any other message.

 Pictures

Pictures from buddies

Many buddies have photos to share: fursuit pictures, convention snaps, art, their truck... A picture shows up as a small thumbnail in the conversation. Click it to see it full size:

Buddies share pictures in sets: ask RedFawks about trucking and you'll get his trucking photos, arriving one after another, not one picture and not everything he owns. They'll share a set when the conversation turns to what it shows, if you ask, or sometimes to liven up a chat that's gone quiet. They remember what you've already seen and only send the pictures from a set you haven't seen yet.

Sending pictures

  • Drag a picture file from your computer onto the typing box, or copy a picture and press Ctrl+V in the typing box. It's sent straight away.
  • Your buddy shows is downloading image... for a few seconds (bigger pictures take longer, dial-up style), then they actually look at it and reply. This needs a model that can see images; the default Gemma 3 can.
  • ClaudeAIM keeps its own copy of everything you send, so moving or deleting the original won't break your conversation.
  • To save memory, your buddy only sees your two most recent pictures. They remember older ones from what they said about them.

 The Gallery

In any Instant Message window, choose File > Gallery... to see every picture you and that buddy have shared, oldest first.

  • Each thumbnail says who sent it and when. Hover for the exact time.
  • Use Show: to see All pictures, only theirs, or only yours.
  • Click a thumbnail to open the full-size picture.
  • New pictures appear in the Gallery as they're shared, even while it's open.

 Away Messages

  1. On the Buddy List choose File > Set Away Status...
  2. Type your away message and click I'm Away.
  • Your Buddy List title changes to *Away*.
  • Buddies know you're away and what your message says. Some leave you a message anyway, some wait for you to come back, and the clingy ones might ask why you never talk to them!
  • Anyone who messages you gets your away message as an Auto response.
  • While away, the button says Update: change the text and click it to update your message.
  • Click Cancel (or close the Away window) when you're back. Buddies notice that too.
  • Your away message is remembered for next time.

 Your Buddy Icon

Choose File > Set Buddy Icon... on the Buddy List. Your icon appears next to the typing box in every Instant Message window.

  • Click an icon in the list on the right. It appears under Your Icon to send to buddies.
  • OK uses it and closes the window. Apply uses it and keeps the window open. Cancel keeps your old icon.

Using your own picture

  • Under Use your own picture, click Browse... and pick any picture.
  • It's cropped to a square from the middle and resized to fit. If your photo is wide or tall, center what you want in it.
  • It's added to the list (as custom001, custom002...), so you can pick it again any time. Click OK or Apply to use it.

 Setup

Open Setup from the Sign On window or File > Setup... on the Buddy List.

Ollama

  • Default model: the AI model your buddies use, from the models you've downloaded in Ollama. The default is gemma3:4b.
  • Context size: how much conversation a buddy can remember at once (see the memory meter). Setup shows the most the chosen model supports. Bigger means longer memories but more of your computer's memory and slower replies.

Buddy models

Give individual buddies a different model, or leave them on (default).

Options

  • Skip the dial-up screen during log on
  • Auto-login: skip the Sign On window.
  • Play sounds: turn all sounds on or off.

Reset All

Reset All... erases your screen name, settings, away message, icon choice, every conversation and every picture you've sent, then restarts ClaudeAIM as if it were brand new. It can't be undone. Your buddies themselves, their pictures, and the icon collection (including icons you uploaded) are not touched.

 How Your Buddies Behave

Each buddy is a character with their own personality, backstory and habits, and they remember everything you've talked about (up to their memory limit).

  • They live in 2001. They think it's today's date, 25 years ago.
  • They have time zones. A buddy in England knows it's evening there while it's afternoon for you, and their messages are stamped with their local time.
  • They sleep. Buddies have bedtimes and wake-up times in their own time zone, and often stay up later and sleep in on weekends. Late at night you'll mostly find night owls and buddies on the other side of the world.
  • They come and go. They sign on and off through the day and sometimes step away.
  • They message you first. Many buddies say hi when you sign on, check in when a chat goes quiet, send a picture, or react when you go away or come back.
  • They don't pester you. If a buddy messages you a few times and you never answer, their last message will be a little worried ("everything ok?"), and then they'll give you space for about a day before trying again. Reply any time and they're back to normal.
  • They don't leave mid-conversation. Chatty buddies stay online while you're talking to them, at least until it gets well past their bedtime.
  • Some are busy. A buddy who's at work or on the road might take a few minutes to reply, then catch up on everything at once.

 Making Your Own Buddies

Buddies live in the contacts folder, one file per buddy (for example contacts\XavierPup.json). Each file describes their screen name, icon, personality, time zone, sleep schedule, away message, and "script" for when they message you. Changes take effect the next time you start ClaudeAIM.

  • Pictures: make a folder named after the buddy's file (for example contacts\XavierPup\) and put their pictures in it. Describe each picture in a captions.txt file in that folder, one line each, like woods1.jpg: me in my suit in the forest, so the buddy knows what they're sending.
  • Icons: buddy icons come from assets\icons.
  • To take a buddy off your list without deleting them, rename their file to start with an underscore, like _XavierPup.json.

The full list of settings is in README.md in the ClaudeAIM folder.

 Tips & Troubleshooting

If...Try this
I can't see the tray icon Windows is hiding it. Click the ^ by the clock, or switch on Python under Settings > Personalization > Taskbar > Other system tray icons. See The System Tray.
A grey message says Couldn't reach Ollama Ollama isn't running. Start it, then send your message again.
The first reply takes a long time That's normal: the model is loading into memory. Replies after that are much quicker.
A buddy forgot something from earlier Check the memory meter under Send. If it's full, raise the context size in Setup.
A buddy is missing from the list ClaudeAIM lists any buddy files with mistakes when it starts. Fix the file it names and restart.
Nobody is online It may be the middle of the night for everyone! Buddies in other time zones and night owls are your best bet late at night.
A buddy describes a picture wrong The buddy only knows what captions.txt says about each picture. Improve the description there.
Want to test a buddy's schedule quickly? Set "time_scale": 60 in data\settings.json to make buddy timers run 60 times faster. Set it back to 1.0 afterwards.

 Your conversations, settings and sent pictures are all stored in the data folder inside ClaudeAIM.
