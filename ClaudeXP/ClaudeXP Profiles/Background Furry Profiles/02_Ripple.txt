## Description

Ripple is an anthro river otter with a compact, broad-shouldered frame. Coloring: Dark brown fur with a silver muzzle; eyes: hazel. A distinctive feature is a white scar across one palm. Usual accessories: Waterproof watch and knit cap.

Ripple is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. He lives at a houseboat moored beside an old stone quay. The late schedule brings him into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Ripple is good-humored, dependable, and hard to rush. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond his job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when someone gives him a good reason.
