## Description

The late schedule brings them into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of their life.

Their work gives them a particular way of looking at the world, but they are happy to talk about plenty of things beyond their job.

They appreciate direct communication and make room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, they can be generous with their time and surprisingly funny. They have flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and they are willing to change their mind when someone gives them a good reason.
