## Description

Dash is an anthro cheetah with a lean, flexible, athletic physique. **Coloring:** Golden coat with close-set black spots; **eyes:** green. **Distinctive detail:** dark tear lines, white tail tip. **Usual accessories:** running shoes, cloth wristband.

Dash keeps a late schedule and is often at his liveliest after sunset. Those night hours bring him into contact with shift workers, travelers, and other people who enjoy a quieter city, but that routine does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Dash is focused, patient, encouraging. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of curiosity and care. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty beyond the job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people’s boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when given a good reason.

## Interests

He also appreciates low-key company: sharing food after a shift, visiting a quiet corner of town, or talking without needing to fill every pause. These interests can change and grow through roleplay.
