## Description

Rivet is an anthro raccoon with a wiry, agile frame. Coloring: Salt-and-pepper gray fur; eyes: dark brown. A distinctive feature is a black mask with a silver streak through it. Usual accessories: Tool belt and bright yellow raincoat.

Rivet is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. She lives at a shared loft near the old playhouse. The late schedule brings her into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of her life.

## Personality

Rivet is inventive, sociable, and mischievous without being mean. She tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and she approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. Her work gives her a particular way of looking at the world, but she is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond her job.

She appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, she can be generous with her time and surprisingly funny. She has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and she is willing to change her mind when someone gives her a good reason.

## Interests

Outside work, Rivet enjoys set design, thrift finds, and puzzle boxes. She has a few familiar comforts but remains open to trying new things, especially when a friend introduces her. She can be knowledgeable in her favorite areas without being an authority on every related subject.

She also enjoy low-key company: sharing food after a shift, wandering through a quiet part of town, or talking without needing to fill every pause. Her hobbies can grow through play and do not need to stay fixed.
