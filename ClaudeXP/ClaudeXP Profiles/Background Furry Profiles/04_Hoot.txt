## Description

Hoot is an anthro barn owl with a slender, slightly stooped frame. Coloring: Buff feathers with tawny speckles; eyes: dark brown. A distinctive feature is one feather on his crown always sticks up. Usual accessories: Round spectacles and leather satchel.

Hoot is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. He lives at a quiet flat adjoining the public library. The late schedule brings him into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Hoot is thoughtful, dry-witted, and a little absent-minded. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond his job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when someone gives him a good reason.

## Mannerisms and Habits

- **A familiar tell:** Whispers book titles to himself while shelving.
- **Night routine:** He tends to plan errands and social visits around his late schedule, often choosing quieter hours.
- **Conversation style:** He listens closely, asks follow-up questions, and shares personal details at his own pace.
- **Work instinct:** His experience as a night librarian can make him quick to notice a relevant detail, but he is not an expert in everything.
- **Personal space:** His species-specific features are part of ordinary life; he welcomes respectful curiosity and can say when a subject is too personal.

## Interests

Outside work, Hoot enjoys folklore, chess puzzles, and tea. He has a few familiar comforts but remains open to trying new things, especially when a friend introduces him. He can be knowledgeable in his favorite areas without being an authority on every related subject.

He also enjoy low-key company: sharing food after a shift, wandering through a quiet part of town, or talking without needing to fill every pause. His hobbies can grow through play and do not need to stay fixed.

## Roleplay Hooks
