## Description

## Personality

Tessellate is methodical, kind, rule-conscious. They tend to notice the small details other people overlook, and they approach new situations with a mix of curiosity and care. Their work gives them a particular way of looking at the world, but they are happy to talk about plenty beyond the job.

They appreciate direct communication and make room for other people’s boundaries. Once comfortable, they can be generous with their time and surprisingly funny. They have flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and they are willing to change their mind when given a good reason.
