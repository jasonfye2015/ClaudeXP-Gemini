## Description

Chroma is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. She lives at a top-floor room filled with drying paper. The late schedule brings her into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of her life.

## Personality

Chroma is precise, diplomatic, and keenly observant. She tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and she approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. Her work gives her a particular way of looking at the world, but she is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond her job.

She appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, she can be generous with her time and surprisingly funny. She has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and she is willing to change her mind when someone gives her a good reason.
