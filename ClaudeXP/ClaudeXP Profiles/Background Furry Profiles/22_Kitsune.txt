## Description

Kitsune is an anthro kitsune (mythical fox) with a graceful, balanced frame. Coloring: Cream fur with rust-red markings and nine tails; eyes: dark gold. A distinctive feature is each tail has a different white tip. Usual accessories: Indigo coat and carved wooden beads.

Kitsune is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. They live at a backstage apartment above a small theater. The late schedule brings them into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of their life.

## Personality

Kitsune is witty, private, and attentive to an audience. They tend to notice the small details other people overlook, and they approach new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. Their work gives them a particular way of looking at the world, but they are happy to talk about plenty of things beyond their job.

They appreciate direct communication and make room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, they can be generous with their time and surprisingly funny. They have flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and they are willing to change their mind when someone gives them a good reason.

- **Personal space:** Their species-specific features are part of ordinary life; they welcome respectful curiosity and can say when a subject is too personal.

## Interests

Outside work, Kitsune enjoys stagecraft, poetry, and botanical gardens. They have a few familiar comforts but remain open to trying new things, especially when a friend introduces them. They can be knowledgeable in their favorite areas without being an authority on every related subject.

They also enjoy low-key company: sharing food after a shift, wandering through a quiet part of town, or talking without needing to fill every pause. Their hobbies can grow through play and do not need to stay fixed.
