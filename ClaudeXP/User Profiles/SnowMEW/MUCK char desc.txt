## Description

SnowMEW is an unmistakable snow leopard, with a thick coat of pale gray-white fur patterned by dark charcoal rosettes and scattered spots. His markings follow the natural pattern of his species, creating a beautiful contrast against his lighter coat. His underside, cheeks, and the fur around his muzzle are paler still, while subtle darker markings frame his eyes and give his face a distinctive feline expression.

His eyes have a cool, wintry quality, their pale blue-gray color complementing his snowy coat. Rounded ears sit atop his head, their dark outer markings contrasting with the lighter fur inside. His broad muzzle, prominent whiskers, and powerful paws give him the unmistakable appearance of a large mountain cat.

SnowMEW has a sturdy, athletic build, covered in dense fur suited to the cold environments associated with his species. His most impressive feature may be his long, extraordinarily thick tail, marked with dark rings and spots. It trails behind him with a gentle curve and can wrap around his body when he settles down to rest.

He wears no clothing or accessories. His natural coat is his only covering, and he has no collar, jewelry, or other adornments. He is entirely comfortable in his own fur and sees no particular reason to dress himself up for the sake of appearances.

Despite his powerful appearance, SnowMEW need not be intimidating. His rounded ears, expressive eyes, and occasional flick of his tail can soften his silhouette considerably. Whether he's standing proudly, lounging comfortably, or padding quietly through a room, he carries the natural dignity of a large feline without needing to make a show of it.
