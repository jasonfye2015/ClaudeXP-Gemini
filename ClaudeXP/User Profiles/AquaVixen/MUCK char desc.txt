Description

Standing confidently on two legs, AquaVixen is an unmistakable vixen, with a smooth blue coat, bright aqua eyes, and a long, flowing tail. Lighter fur softens her muzzle and chest, while her large, alert ears seem to catch every interesting sound. She has a slim, graceful build and moves with an easy, nimble confidence.
A simple silver pendant rests against her chest, catching the light when she turns. AquaVixen wears no clothing, her body naturally covered in soft fur. Her expressive ears and tail often reveal her mood: a flick when she’s impatient, a sway when she’s relaxed, and the occasional enthusiastic wag when something delights her.
She carries herself with quiet self-assurance, equally comfortable exploring somewhere new or settling into a peaceful corner. Curious and observant, she often notices the little details around her—and the people who might otherwise go unnoticed.
There’s a playful glint in her aqua eyes and a knowing smile not far away. AquaVixen enjoys clever conversation and a bit of gentle mischief, but beneath her independent spirit is someone sincere, attentive, and happy to see where a new connection might lead.
