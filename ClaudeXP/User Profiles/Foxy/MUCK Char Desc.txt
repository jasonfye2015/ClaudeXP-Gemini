Description

Standing lean and agile on two legs, Foxy is a sharp, handsome Red Fox with a vibrant reddish-orange coat, striking white-and-cream accents along his chest and muzzle, and sleek black fur outlining his ears and lower legs. His build is athletic yet relaxed, given a plush volume by his soft, dense fur. His large, pointed ears are exceptionally expressive, constantly swiveling toward subtle sounds or betraying his curiosity long before he speaks. His warm amber eyes hold a sharp, intelligent spark, complemented by a frequent, knowing smirk.

Resting against his back is a long, remarkably bushy tail tipped with a prominent white brush, which sways gently as he walks or flicks when a sudden thought strikes him. His only accessory is a thin leather cord worn around his neck, holding a small brass compass that catches the light whenever he shifts.

Foxy wears no clothing, his body fully covered by his natural fur. He carries himself with a quiet, nimble confidence, equally at ease navigating a crowded gathering or leaning comfortably against a tree frame at the edge of a quiet trail.

Despite his keen, observant demeanor, Foxy isn't overly serious or distant. He's a fox through and through, with plenty of room for quick wit, warmth, easygoing banter, and a playful sense of adventure beneath that bushy tail.
