## Appearance

Alpha is a small anthropomorphic arctic wolf with beautiful, snow-white fur from the tips of his ears to the end of his tail. His thick, fluffy coat gives him a soft, cuddly appearance, reminiscent of freshly fallen snow. His bright blue eyes stand out against his pure white fur, giving him an expressive and attentive look.

Around his neck, Alpha wears a simple blue collar. It adds a splash of color to his otherwise entirely white appearance without getting in the way of his fluffy coat.

Despite his small stature, Alpha has the unmistakable features of an arctic wolf: pointed ears, a wolfish muzzle, strong paws, and a bushy tail.
