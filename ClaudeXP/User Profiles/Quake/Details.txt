Quake
A Resonant Hyena — Character Profile
Full Name: Quake Vance
Real Name: Arthur Pendelton
DOB: 8/12/1975
Species: Anthropomorphic Spotted Hyena
Gender: Male
Build: Tall, lanky but wiry, built for endurance and a heavy, grounding stance
Fur: Tawny, golden-brown base with dark, irregular spots and a coarse, darker ridge down his spine
Eyes: Bright, piercing amber
Distinguishing Features: A thick, blunt-tipped tail, large rounded highly mobile ears, a scruffy silver-tipped mane.
Accessories: A thick braided leather collar adorned with polished bone charms and a single large raven feather
Occupation: Freelance Tech Support
Residence: New York
Sleep Schedule: 8pm-4am 

Personality
Quake is inherently social and boisterous, with a presence that feels more inviting and lively than intimidating. He enjoys a crowd and appreciates the loud, shared joys of life: a roaring campfire, a good belly laugh, a lively debate, or the simple comfort of being surrounded by his found-pack.
He's highly observant of the people he travels with and tends to be fascinated by their quirks and stories. He's a fantastic listener when someone needs to vent, but he also thrives on playful banter and firmly believes that no journey is complete without a few terrible jokes.
His manner is generally relaxed and slouching, exuding a loose, easygoing confidence. He isn't without his stubborn streaks, however, and when something truly catches his interest or sparks his competitive side, his ears and tail betray his excitement long before he lets it show.
Quake values loyalty, honesty, and the strength of his pack. He doesn't expect everyone to be the same, nor does he believe his rugged, shadowy appearance makes him any tougher or more cynical than he actually is. He would much rather be known for his fierce loyalty and ready laugh than for his scruffy exterior.
Mannerisms and Habits

    The tail: His blunt-tipped tail swishes with restless energy when he's thinking, droops low when he's exhausted, and wags in wide, sweeping arcs when he's genuinely happy.
    The ears: His large, rounded ears swivel independently to catch distant sounds. They perk up sharply at the punchline of a joke, and flatten slightly against his head when he's embarrassed or caught off guard.
    The collar: The polished bone charms on his leather collar clack together with a soft, rhythmic clink-clack when he walks or shifts his weight, providing a grounding, familiar sound to those near him.
    The laugh: True to his name, his laughter is a deep, resonant, rumbling chuckle that literally vibrates in his chest and can be felt by anyone sitting close to him.
    Personal space: Being a deeply pack-oriented creature, he is very comfortable with friendly proximity. He often leans against his friends, sits shoulder-to-shoulder, or rests a hand on a companion's arm during conversation.

Interests
Quake is always eager to discover new trails, ruins, and hidden spots through the people and places he encounters. He might enjoy tracking through the wilderness, scavenging for lost artifacts, sharing exaggerated stories late into the evening, or finding a comfortable, elevated perch to watch the world go by.
He has a particular appreciation for camaraderie and the sort of conversations that bounce from one topic to another over a shared meal. A little friendly competition, a curious question, or an unexpected detour can be enough to make an otherwise ordinary day memorable.
He's also quite capable of enjoying peaceful solitude. Sometimes the best company is the sound of the wind through the canyons, the rustle of his wings, and the occasional soft clinking of his collar charms.
Roleplay Hooks
Quake is an open-ended character, so other players have plenty of ways to approach him.

    A boisterous companion: His loud laugh and ready smile naturally invite others to join in the fun, breaking the tension in heavy situations.
    A skilled tracker: He can read the ground like a book, making him an invaluable guide for finding lost things, lost people, or hidden paths.
    A scavenger with a find: He always seems to have a pocket full of weird trinkets, a half-finished map, or a rumor about something interesting just over the next ridge.
    A loyal packmate: He is fiercely protective of his friends. Someone in need of backup or a listening ear will find Quake standing firmly by their side.
    A grounding presence: When things get chaotic or emotional, his deep, resonant voice and steady, slouching demeanor can help calm the room.
    
Roleplay Notes
Quake is intended to be a collaborative character rather than the center of every scene. He can initiate conversations, crack jokes, make mistakes, and lead the charge on an adventure, while leaving other characters plenty of room to respond and influence events.
His rugged, shadowy appearance and bat wings don't automatically make him an edgy loner, a brooding anti-hero, or morally gray. He is a deeply social, warm-hearted creature at his core. His physical traits are distinctive, not a claim to being the "coolest" or most mysterious person in the room.
He responds to the world around him, and his relationships develop through shared experiences, shared meals, and shared laughs. Friends become friends over time, trust is earned on the road, and even a brief encounter around a campfire can be worthwhile without needing to turn into a grand adventure.
In short: Quake is a tall, lanky, tawny spotted hyena, bright amber eyes, and a leather collar of clinking bone charms. He's boisterous, fiercely loyal, and ready to share a laugh or a long journey.
