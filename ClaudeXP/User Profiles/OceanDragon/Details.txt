# OceanDragon
*The Emerald Dragon — Character Profile*

**Full Name:** OceanDragon  
**Real Name:** Thomas Henderson
**DOB:** 10/21/1980
**Species:** Anthropomorphic Dragon  
**Gender:** Male  
**Height:** Average  
**Build:** Sturdy and well-proportioned, with a natural dragon's physique  
**Scales:** Rich green  
**Eyes:** Deep blue  
**Horns:** White  
**Distinguishing Features:** A white, furry mohawk-like crest running from the back of his head down his spine, continuing along his tail and ending in a large, fluffy white tuft at the tip  
**Claws:** White claws on both hands and feet  
**Accessories:** None  
**Clothing:** None  
**Occupation:** None
**Sleep Schedule:** Midnight - 7AM 
**Residence:** Wherever he feels at home

## Personality

OceanDragon is a character whose personality can be as distinctive as his appearance. He has a calm, approachable presence, with enough confidence to be comfortable around unfamiliar people without feeling the need to dominate a conversation.

He's naturally curious about the world around him. New places, unusual characters, and interesting conversations can all capture his attention. He enjoys learning what makes other people tick, especially when their experiences differ from his own.

OceanDragon can be thoughtful and easygoing, appreciating conversations that develop naturally rather than feeling compelled to fill every silence. He doesn't mind a little quiet, and he can be just as content sharing a peaceful moment with a friend as he is participating in a lively gathering.

There is also room for a playful side. He may enjoy good-natured teasing, amusing stories, or a bit of friendly competition. His distinctive crest and fluffy tail tip might even become the subject of occasional jokes, particularly among friends who have grown comfortable with one another.

He values being treated as an individual rather than being judged by his appearance. Being a dragon doesn't automatically make him fearsome, ancient, magical, or particularly wise. Those qualities, if they belong to him at all, should emerge from his own history and experiences.

Above all, OceanDragon is open to discovery. He can form friendships, develop new interests, and encounter situations that reveal unexpected sides of his character.

## Mannerisms and Habits

- **The white crest:** His furry mohawk may shift and fluff slightly as he moves. When he stretches or turns his head, the fur along his neck can ripple gently.
- **The tail tuft:** The large white puff at the end of his tail is hard to miss. It may sway behind him, curl around his side when he rests, or bounce lightly as he walks.
- **The horns:** His white horns give his head a distinctive silhouette. He may angle his head when listening or turn carefully when moving through a narrow space.
- **The eyes:** His deep blue eyes tend to focus on whoever is speaking, especially when something genuinely interests him.
- **The claws:** His white claws occasionally click against a hard surface or make a faint scraping sound when he adjusts his grip. He generally takes care not to scratch things accidentally.
- **The posture:** He tends to stand comfortably rather than rigidly, shifting his weight from one foot to the other when relaxed.
- **The crest fluff:** The white fur along his back and tail can be ruffled by a breeze or disturbed when he settles against a chair. He may occasionally smooth it back into place.
- **Personal space:** He's comfortable around others but respects their boundaries, especially when his horns, claws, or tail might accidentally get in the way.

## Interests

OceanDragon enjoys having the freedom to explore different interests without being confined to a single stereotype of what a dragon should enjoy.

He may appreciate water, coastlines, rivers, or the deep blue of the ocean, though his name need not dictate his entire personality or history. Perhaps he enjoys the sound of waves, or perhaps he prefers quiet woodland paths, city lights, or the company of friends in a comfortable gathering place.

He may enjoy creative pursuits, games, technology, music, or simply learning about the lives of other inhabitants. He appreciates a good conversation and can become absorbed in a topic when it catches his interest.

He also understands the value of relaxation. A comfortable place to sit, a peaceful evening, and time to let his thoughts wander can be every bit as enjoyable as a grand adventure.

His exact hobbies remain open-ended so they can emerge naturally through roleplay. He doesn't need to have every interest decided in advance; discovering new things can be part of his character's ongoing story.

## Roleplay Hooks

OceanDragon's unusual appearance and flexible personality offer several ways for other characters to meet and interact with him.

- **The unusual dragon:** Someone notices his white furry crest and asks how it feels, how he cares for it, or whether it has any special significance.
- **The curious acquaintance:** He encounters someone with an interesting story and asks a few thoughtful questions.
- **The quiet companion:** Another character discovers that OceanDragon is perfectly happy to share a peaceful space without requiring constant conversation.
- **The friendly challenger:** A game, puzzle, or good-natured competition gives him an opportunity to show a more energetic side.
- **The unexpected explorer:** A curious discovery sends him and another character somewhere unfamiliar.
- **The crest conversation:** His unusual combination of green scales and white fur becomes the starting point for a conversation about dragon anatomy, personal style, or character lore.
- **The old friend:** He reconnects with someone after a long absence and discovers how much has changed.
- **The emerging mystery:** His name, appearance, or personal history might eventually lead to a story, but nothing about him needs to be supernatural unless that becomes part of his chosen lore.

## Roleplay Notes

OceanDragon is intended to be a collaborative character with room for growth. He should be able to initiate conversations, express preferences, make mistakes, and respond meaningfully to the actions of other characters.

His dragon anatomy is part of his everyday life. His horns, claws, scales, and furry crest may occasionally affect how he moves through the world, but they don't need to be mentioned in every pose.

His appearance doesn't automatically grant him magical abilities, flight, fire-breathing, extraordinary strength, or immortality. Those details can be established separately if they're appropriate for the world in which he lives.

His white crest and tail tuft are distinctive physical traits, not a substitute for personality. He can be serious, funny, shy, confident, curious, or thoughtful depending on the situation. His behavior should grow from his experiences rather than from assumptions about dragons.

OceanDragon's lack of clothing is a normal aspect of his character design. His scales provide his natural covering, and he doesn't need to wear accessories to feel complete.

His relationships develop through shared experiences. Friends can tease him affectionately about his fluffy tail, challenge him to a game, invite him on an adventure, or simply enjoy his company. He should leave other characters plenty of room to contribute to the story.

**In short:** OceanDragon is an average-height green anthro dragon with deep blue eyes, white horns, and a remarkable white furry crest running from the back of his head down his spine and along his tail, ending in a fluffy white puff. Unadorned and comfortable in his natural form, he's an approachable dragon ready to explore the world and meet the characters who inhabit it.
