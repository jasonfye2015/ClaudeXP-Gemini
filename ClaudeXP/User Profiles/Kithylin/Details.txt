# Kithylin
*An Angelic Husky — Character Profile*

**Full Name:** Kithylin Perth
**Real Name:** Jason Williams
**DOB:** 4/25/1978
**Species:** Anthropomorphic Husky  
**Gender:** Male  
**Build:** Tall, sturdy, and comfortably fluffy  
**Fur:** Traditional husky black-and-white markings  
**Eyes:** Bright blue  
**Distinguishing Features:** A pair of magnificent white angelic wings, a softly glowing golden halo, and a thick, fluffy husky tail with a natural curl  
**Accessories:** A blue collar with a small yellow bell  
**Occupation:** Unemployed 
**Residence:** Wherever the next adventure takes him
**Real Live Residence:** Upstate New York
**Sleep Schedule**: Midnight-7am

## Personality

Kithylin is approachable by nature, with a presence that feels more welcoming than imposing. He enjoys good company and appreciates the little things: a pleasant conversation, a shared laugh, a peaceful walk, or the simple pleasure of being around others without needing to fill every silence.

He's curious about the people he meets and tends to be interested in what makes each individual unique. He can be a good listener when someone needs to talk, but he also appreciates playful banter and doesn't believe every conversation needs to become profound.

His manner is generally gentle and easygoing, though he isn't without his own opinions, preferences, or stubborn husky moments. He can be enthusiastic when something catches his interest, and his ears and tail have a habit of expressing excitement even when he's attempting to act composed.

Kithylin values kindness, honesty, and companionship. He doesn't expect everyone to be alike, nor does he believe that having wings or a halo makes him better than anyone else. He would much rather be known for how he treats others than for how he looks.

## Mannerisms and Habits

- **The tail:** His fluffy tail tends to curl more tightly when he's pleased, sway when he's relaxed, and wag enthusiastically when he forgets to play it cool.
- **The ears:** They perk up at interesting sounds, swivel toward whoever is speaking, and sometimes flatten slightly when he's embarrassed or caught off guard.
- **The bell:** The little yellow bell on his blue collar gives a delicate jingle when he walks, shifts his posture, or gets particularly animated.
- **The wings:** His white wings occasionally stretch or fluff themselves out, especially after sitting for a while. He may fold them neatly against his back or open them slightly when expressing himself.
- **The halo:** Its warm golden glow is a constant feature of his appearance, lending a soft radiance to his silhouette.
- **Personal space:** He's comfortable with friendly company, but lets new acquaintances establish their own comfort levels rather than assuming everyone wants immediate closeness.

## Interests

Kithylin is happy to discover new interests through the people and places he encounters. He might enjoy exploring wooded trails, watching the world from a quiet overlook, sharing stories late into the evening, or finding a comfortable place to relax after a long day.

He has a particular appreciation for friendly company and the sort of conversations that wander from one subject to another without anyone watching the clock. A little humor, a curious question, or an unexpected adventure can be enough to make an otherwise ordinary day memorable.

He's also quite capable of enjoying peaceful solitude. Sometimes the best company is the sound of wind through the trees, the rustle of feathers, and the occasional soft jingle of his collar bell.

## Roleplay Hooks

Kithylin is an open-ended character, so other players have plenty of ways to approach him.

- **A curious stranger:** His wings and halo naturally invite questions, though his answers needn't be mysterious or dramatic.
- **A new friend:** He welcomes conversation and enjoys learning about the lives and personalities of others.
- **An outdoor companion:** A walk through the forest, a flight above the treetops, or a quiet evening under the stars could become the beginning of a story.
- **A comforting presence:** Someone having a difficult day might find Kithylin willing to listen, offer company, or simply sit with them in comfortable silence.
- **A playful encounter:** His expressive ears, fluffy tail, and little bell provide plenty of opportunities for lighthearted interaction.
- **An unfolding story:** His origins, the nature of his wings, and whatever significance his halo might hold can remain mysteries until a story gives him a reason to explore them.

## Roleplay Notes

Kithylin is intended to be a collaborative character rather than the center of every scene. He can initiate conversations, express opinions, make mistakes, and have adventures, while leaving other characters room to respond and influence events.

His angelic appearance doesn't automatically make him all-knowing, invulnerable, or morally perfect. His wings and halo are distinctive physical traits, not a claim to authority over other characters.

He responds to the world around him, and his relationships develop through shared experiences. Friends become friends over time, trust is earned, and even a brief encounter can be worthwhile without needing to turn into a grand adventure.

**In short:** Kithylin is a tall, fluffy, blue-eyed husky with white wings, a golden halo, and a little bell on his blue collar. He's warm, curious, approachable, and ready to see where a conversation leads.
