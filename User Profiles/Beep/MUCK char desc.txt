## Description

Beep is an average-height protogen with the distinctive silhouette of a wolf and the unmistakable features of a cybernetic being. Their appearance combines organic fur with advanced technology, creating a character who feels equally at home in a digital environment or a casual gathering with other inhabitants of the world.

Their fur is gray and grizzled, with subtle variations in tone that give it a weathered, lived-in appearance. Rather than looking perfectly uniform, their coat has the sort of natural irregularity that makes it feel real. The fur around their neck and shoulders may be a little thicker, while their limbs and face have shorter, more practical coverage.

Their most unusual organic feature is a pair of pairs of velvety wolf ears. The four ears sit in two distinct sets, each covered in soft gray fur with a plush texture. They can move independently or respond together to different sounds, giving Beep an expressive silhouette. One pair might turn toward a conversation while the other swivels toward a distant noise, occasionally making their attention look delightfully divided.

Their protogen visor is a smooth, dark display with a completely black background and white as its primary display color. Simple white shapes and symbols stand out sharply against the darkness, making their expressions visible without requiring a conventional organic face. Their display can communicate emotion through eyes, symbols, and other visual indicators, allowing them to convey everything from curiosity to amusement.

Beep has the standard enhancements associated with a protogen: a cybernetic visor and facial assembly, integrated technological components, and the usual combination of organic and mechanical anatomy. Their design can include the customary technological augmentations of the species, such as reinforced cybernetic components and integrated interfaces, while leaving the precise hardware configuration open to the conventions used in ClaudeXP.

Their gray fur and monochrome display complement each other. The contrast between the weathered organic coat and the crisp white graphics gives Beep a recognizable appearance without requiring elaborate colors or unnecessary decoration.
