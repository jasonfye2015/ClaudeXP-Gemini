Description

Standing tall on two legs, Kithylin is an unmistakably husky fellow, with a handsome black-and-white coat, expressive blue eyes, and a thick, gloriously fluffy tail that curls behind him. His build is sturdy and substantial, softened by the plush appearance of his fur. His ears are wonderfully expressive, often betraying his mood before he says a word.

A pair of magnificent white wings rests upon his back, their feathers soft and immaculate. Above his head floats a golden halo, casting a gentle, warm glow over his ears and forehead. Neither feature seems to demand attention; they are simply part of who he is. His only accessory is a blue collar with a little yellow bell, which gives a cheerful jingle whenever he moves.

Kithylin wears no clothing, his body naturally covered in thick fur. He carries himself with a relaxed confidence, equally comfortable standing proudly in the middle of a busy gathering or curling up somewhere quiet to enjoy a peaceful moment.

Despite his angelic appearance, Kithylin isn't necessarily a solemn or otherworldly figure. He's a husky, after all, and there's plenty of room for warmth, curiosity, playfulness, and the occasional bit of mischief beneath those snowy wings.
