## Description

OceanDragon is an anthropomorphic dragon with rich green scales and deep blue eyes that stand out vividly against his emerald coloring. His physique is comfortably average in height, with a balanced build that gives him a natural, grounded presence. He has the unmistakable features of a dragon: a scaled body, a long tail, clawed hands and feet, and a pair of striking white horns.

His horns curve upward from his head, pale against the green scales surrounding their base. They frame his face without overwhelming it, giving him a distinctive silhouette. His deep blue eyes are expressive and attentive, capable of conveying curiosity, amusement, or quiet thought with only a subtle change in expression.

OceanDragon's most unusual feature is his magnificent white fur crest. Beginning at the back of his head, it forms a thick, fluffy mohawk that follows the line of his neck and continues down his back. The soft fur contrasts beautifully with his smooth green scales, creating a distinctive ridge of white against emerald. The crest continues along his tail, where it culminates in a generous, cloudlike puff of white fur at the very tip.

The furry crest isn't merely a small patch of decoration. It is an unmistakable part of his appearance, giving him a unique combination of reptilian and mammalian textures. Depending on the light and the way he moves, the soft white fur may appear almost snowy against his green scales.

His hands and feet end in neat, hard white claws. They provide a sharp visual contrast to his scales and are an ordinary part of his anatomy. He may use them to grip objects, climb, or simply gesture while speaking, though he can be careful with them around more delicate surroundings.

OceanDragon wears no clothing or accessories. His scales, horns, white claws, and unusual furry crest are all the adornment he needs. He is comfortable in his natural appearance and carries himself without any need for additional decoration.
