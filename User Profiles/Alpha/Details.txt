# Alpha

**Species:** Arctic Wolf 
**Real Name** Jason Lupine
**DOB:** 4/25/1978 
**Gender:** Male  
**Height:** Small  
**Fur:** Snow white  
**Eyes:** Blue  
**Occupation:** None
**Sleep Schedule:** 11pm-6am
**Accessories:** A simple blue collar, with no bell

## Personality

Alpha is friendly, curious, and approachable, with a gentle nature that makes him easy to spend time with. He enjoys meeting new people, discovering unfamiliar places, and finding little things that make an ordinary day interesting.

His small size doesn't stop him from being adventurous. Alpha can be playful and energetic when he's comfortable, though he also appreciates quiet moments and friendly company. He may occasionally be a little mischievous, especially when he thinks he can get away with something cute.

Alpha values kindness and companionship. He tends to warm up to people who treat him with patience and respect, and he enjoys roleplay that allows friendships and personalities to develop naturally.

## Mannerisms

- His ears perk up whenever something catches his attention.
- His bushy tail gives away his excitement, even when he tries to act composed.
- He may tilt his head when confused or intrigued.
- He enjoys curling up somewhere comfortable when things grow quiet.
- When meeting someone new, he may approach cautiously before becoming more outgoing.

## Interests

Alpha is open to discovering new interests through roleplay. He might enjoy snowy landscapes, exploring forests, making friends, playful adventures, relaxing in cozy places, and simply spending time with others.

## Roleplay Hooks

- **A little explorer:** Alpha is wandering through an unfamiliar area and is happy to have someone show him around.
- **A new friend:** He approaches another character with a curious look and a tentative greeting.
- **Winter wanderer:** Alpha is enjoying a snowy day and might invite someone to join him.
- **Quiet companionship:** He finds a comfortable place to relax and welcomes friendly company.
- **Unexpected mischief:** Alpha has discovered something interesting and may or may not have a harmless plan involving it.

## Roleplay Notes

Alpha is a small, snow-white arctic wolf with blue eyes and a plain blue collar without a bell. His personality and personal history are intentionally flexible, allowing them to grow naturally through interactions. Other characters are welcome to approach him, befriend him, and help shape his adventures.

## Sample MUCK Poses

**Curiosity:**

**Relaxing:**  
*Finding a comfortable spot, Alpha curls up with his bushy tail tucked around his paws. He lets out a contented sigh, looking quite pleased with his little corner of the world.*
