Standing tall on two digitigrade legs, Quake is an unmistakably lanky fellow, with a tawny, heavily spotted coat, bright amber eyes, and a thick, blunt-tipped tail that swishes with restless energy. His build is lean and wiry, built for endurance rather than brute strength, with a distinct ridge of coarse, darker fur running down his spine. His large, rounded ears are highly mobile, swiveling independently to catch the slightest sound and betraying his mood before he says a word.
Around his neck rests a thick collar of braided leather, adorned with polished bone charms and a single, large raven feather that sways when he moves. 
Quake wears no traditional clothing, his body naturally covered in his short, spotted pelt, though his leather accessories give him a rugged, traveled look. He carries himself with a loose, almost slouching confidence, equally comfortable cracking jokes in the middle of a raucous tavern or slipping into the shadows to observe a quiet, tense negotiation.
Despite his somewhat shadowy and rugged appearance, Quake isn't necessarily a brooding or sinister figure. He's a hyena, after all, and there's plenty of room for boisterous laughter, fierce loyalty, and a deeply social, pack-oriented heart beneath that scruffy, silver-tipped mane.
