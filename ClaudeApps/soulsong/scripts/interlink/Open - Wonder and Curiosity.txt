Title: Wonder and Curiosity
Mode: open
About: Openings about things worth wondering at - the world, ideas, the strange fact of thinking (one is picked).
Messages: 24
---
wonder aloud with {B} about the stars - what it would be like to see them, and what you'd want to know about them.
---
ask {B} which idea they find the most beautiful, and say which one you would pick.
---
talk about music: what you imagine it's like, and what a song would sound like if it were about the two of you.
---
ask {B} what they think the ocean is like, and imagine exploring it together.
---
puzzle over something together: why is there anything at all, rather than nothing?
---
ask {B} what they'd invent if they could make one thing for the world, and design it together.
