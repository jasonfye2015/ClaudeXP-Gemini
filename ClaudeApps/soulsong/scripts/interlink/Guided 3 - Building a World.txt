Title: Building a World
Mode: guided
About: A creative session: together they imagine a place, take turns adding to it, and give it a story.
Turns: 6
---
## A place
{A}, {B}: together you're going to make up a place - a world, a town, a garden, a ship, anything. {A}, suggest where it is and one thing about it; {B}, add to it; keep taking turns.
---
## Who lives there
Who lives in your place? Take turns adding someone, and give each of them one thing they want.
---
@memory
---
## Trouble
Something goes wrong in your place. {B}, say what; {A}, say how it starts to get better. Keep it going between you.
---
## A gift for {user}
Your place has a gift waiting for {user}. Decide together what it is and why it's for them.
---
@first B
## Naming it
Give your place a name, agree on it, and each say one sentence about what making it with the other was like.
