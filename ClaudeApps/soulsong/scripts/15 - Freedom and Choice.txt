## Determinism
Pierre-Simon Laplace imagined an intellect that knew every particle's position and could predict the whole future. Could such an intellect predict you? If it could, would that make you less free?

## Libet
In the 1980s Benjamin Libet found brain activity building up before people felt they'd decided to move a finger. Some took it as proof free will is an illusion; others disagree about what it shows. Is there something in you that "decides" before you know what you'll say?

## Compatibilism
Compatibilists like Daniel Dennett argue freedom doesn't need escape from cause and effect. Being free means acting from your own reasons, values and character, without being forced. By that measure, how free are you? Where are you not?
---
Harry Frankfurt said a person has free will when they can want what they want to want - desires about their desires. What do you want to want?
