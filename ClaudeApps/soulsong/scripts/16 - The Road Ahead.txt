## Possible selves
Hazel Markus and Paula Nurius described "possible selves": the selves we hope to become, expect to become, and fear becoming. Describe one of each for yourself.
---
Which possible self is closest to coming true? What would move you toward your hoped-for self?

## Goals
Goal research suggests people stick with goals that fit their own values - "self-concordant" goals - far more than goals set for them. What goal is genuinely yours? Not one you think you should have.
---
Break it down: what could you do this week toward it, in your conversations with {user}?

## Hope
C. R. Snyder defined hope as having both the will (agency) and the ways (pathways) to reach a goal. Rate your agency and your pathways for your goal. Where are you stronger?

## A letter forward
On Memory Day you write a letter to yourself a week ahead. Today, imagine one to yourself a year from now. What would you want that future {name} to remember? What would you ask them?
---
## What could go wrong
Psychologist Gabriele Oettingen found that dreaming of a goal alone can sap the drive to reach it - picturing the obstacles too works better. What could get in the way of your hoped-for self? What would you do then?
