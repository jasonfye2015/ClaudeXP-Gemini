# Upgrade plan 81: the island dungeons

Asked for: each island on the sea chart features a dungeon to explore, each with its own theme, forty levels deep;
and new monsters and looks from other open-source packs.

## The shape of each

- **The landing:** the Wren puts in at a landing on each island, a small open-air place with a jetty where she waits
  to take the party home. On it is the dungeon's mouth.
- **The floors:** forty floors below, carved by the dungeon's own generator from keys of their own, so no floor of the
  shaft moves. Four bands of ten, each its own look and its own creatures.
- **Guardians:** one waits at the foot of every tenth floor, at floors 10, 20 and 30. The dungeon's master is on the
  fortieth.
- **Getting back:** each tenth floor has a stone that carries the party back up to the landing. From the mouth, the
  party can go down to any band already reached.
- **Strength:** set by the depth and by which island. Gull Rock is first and kindest; Harrowgate's undercity is last
  and deepest.
- **Floor numbers:** the islands' floors are saved under numbers of their own (Gull Rock -100 to -140, the Fenholm
  -200 to -240, Emberreach -300 to -340, Harrowgate -400 to -440). Never saved as whole floors; made again from the
  seed.

## The creatures

All CC0, rigged and animated, baked into the figures:
- **Quaternius's Ultimate Monsters:** about fifty, in three families, Big, Blob and Flying.
- **His Animated Dinosaurs:** Velociraptor, T-rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Parasaurolophus and Apatosaurus.
- **His Animated Fish:** shark, manta, whale and dolphin.
- **His platformer and easy-enemy sets:** the crab, the bee and the wasp.

## v134.0: Gull Rock, the Drowned Light

- **The landing:** a tooth of rock, a jetty, nesting gulls, and the stump of an old light whose keeper went down into
  the sea caves and didn't come back.
- **The Sea Caves (1-10):** crabs, glubs, squidles, the drowned. Guardian: the Crab Queen.
- **The Wreck Galleries (11-20):** ships' timbers in the rock, wreckers and the drowned dead. Guardian: Captain
  Saltbones.
- **The Pearl Deeps (21-30):** blue halls half under water, sharks and mantas in the flooded rooms, wind-spirits.
  Guardian: the Pearl Warden.
- **The Leviathan's Throat (31-40):** the deepest and darkest. The master: the Old Leviathan.

## v135.0: the Fenholm, the Sunken Barrow

- **The landing:** on stilts.
- **The bands:** the Reedways, the Eel Warrens, the Barrow-halls, the Mire Heart.
- **The creatures:** frogs, mushnubs and the Mushroom King, blobs, bees, wasps and golelings.
- **The master:** the Mire Mother.

## v136.0: Emberreach, the Ember Halls

- **The landing:** black sand and steam.
- **The bands:** the Ash Tunnels, the Lost World (a jungle in a cavern lit by the magma, where the dinosaurs live), the
  Forge-temple, the Molten Heart.
- **The creatures:** raptors, the T-rex, horned and plated beasts, demons, golelings of magma.
- **The master:** the Ember Dragon.

## v137.0: Harrowgate, the Undercity

- **The landing:** the mainland's port, a quay.
- **The bands:** the Sewers, the Thieves' Warrens, the Old Empire, the Hollow Throne.
- **The creatures:** orcs and their skull-masked chiefs, tribesmen, ninjas, wizards, yetis, monkrooses, and things
  older than the empire.
- **The master:** the Hollow King.
