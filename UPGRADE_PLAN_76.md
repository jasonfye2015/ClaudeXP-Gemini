# UPGRADE_PLAN_76 - The sound of the account

From the audio review (asked for: a sound director's pass - atmosphere, identity, feedback; recommendations with what
is heard, when and why, what it improves, the assets and the cost; then built incrementally). The game's audio is
already broad and all made in code (the bands' consorts, the guardians' themes, the town by the hour, tension that
rises as monsters near, voices for every kind and hero, headphone space, rooms that ring, distant sounds, weather and
fire, a mixer). So the work is repetition, crowding, silence, and the story's own sound - all with the synthesis that
is there, no new files.

## Order (approved: the suggested order)
1. **The mix**: variation on every effect (pitch and level, the bells and fanfares kept true); bursts thinned; a gentle
   compressor on both outputs; silence where it matters - music and ambience dip under conversation, the music falls
   away when a hero falls, a breath of quiet before a new band's theme, a hush on the king's floor. Always eased.
2. **The bells of Caer Duin**: bells almost absent until the story earns them - a cracked bell far off in the Foundry,
   cut short - then at the quiet ending and the Last Page a single bell rung clear; after it, bells in the town's
   music and on the title. With it, each band's first phrase played once as the party arrives.
3. **Claude's sound and the new systems'**: the quill under his lines, a page with each chapter card, the cabin's
   clock, kettle, hearth and cat; a heartbeat when a hero is low in a fight and a small cue for each ailment; the vows'
   seal, the king's wax, the revenants calling in their own voices; weightier crits and falls.

Volume, mute and the headphones stay as they are. Each release checked in the page itself: the graph, the levels, the
moments.
