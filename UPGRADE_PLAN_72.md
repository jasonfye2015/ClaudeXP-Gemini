# UPGRADE_PLAN_72 - Claude, more alive

Asked for: the additions suggested after plan 71, all of them - the last gaps in plan 71, and five ideas to make
Claude feel present and attentive.

## Release 1 - he asks, and remembers
- **His questions.** At the cabin, now and then, "Claude has a question for you": which of them you trust most, why
  you go down, what you're afraid of, what you'll do if you win. One a visit; "I'd rather not say" is always an
  answer. The answers are kept with the village.
- **He uses them.** The next run's opening ("They told me they go down to see what's there"); his account of each run
  (the one you trust most, whether they came home; what you said winning would mean); a line in the margin when what
  you feared comes - a hero lost, or the king's floor reached.
- **His account on the ending's screen**, under the ending's own words, as well as in the Hall of Fame.
- **The court remembers** the party in the second edition: each of the five seated dead has a line for the ones who
  brought the scribe down.
- **Red ink's last gaps**: "open" opens every village building too; a red-ink win no longer announces an Ascension
  rung it doesn't give.

## Release 2 - the world around him
- **The memorial wall** at the shaft's head in the Keep-yard: the names of the fallen, cut small. Space reads one; once
  you've met Claude, it is his hand and his line about each of them.
- **Notes on the desk.** Some days, late morning to mid-afternoon, Claude is out - ink, reeds, a walk. A note waits on
  the desk instead, and sometimes a small errand (a torch, a ration, a healing draught); bring it next time for a small
  thank-you. Never on the first days of a village.
- **Seasons and festivals in the cabin.** The tea changes with the season; he mentions the weather you came in
  through; at a festival there's something of it in the cabin and in what he says.

## Release 3 - seen walking
- When Claude comes down with the party (plan 71's last band), he is drawn walking behind the back row, the way the
  porter and the hound are.

Each release: tests, harness shots, the usual ship.
