## Description

Wolfy is an anthro dire wolf (hypothetical) with a large, muscular frame. Coloring: Dark charcoal fur with a silver ruff; eyes: amber. A distinctive feature is oversized canines and a healed shoulder scar. Usual accessories: Old field jacket and pocket notebook.

Wolfy is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. He lives at a converted farmhouse near the archive. The late schedule brings him into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Wolfy is reserved, scholarly, and unexpectedly warm. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. His past experiences shape his point of view, but he is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond work.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when someone gives him a good reason.

## Interests
