## Description

Nocturne is an anthro bat (fruit bat) with a narrow, delicate-winged frame. Coloring: Dark umber fur and leathery wings; eyes: red-brown. A distinctive feature is large ears with a tiny gold cuff. Usual accessories: Dark scarf and folding cane.

Nocturne is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. He lives at a soundproof flat above a recording studio. The late schedule brings him into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Nocturne is reflective, courteous, and quietly passionate. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond his job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when someone gives him a good reason.

- **Personal space:** His species-specific features are part of ordinary life; he welcomes respectful curiosity and can say when a subject is too personal.

## Interests

Outside work, Nocturne enjoys field recordings, opera, and night markets. He has a few familiar comforts but remains open to trying new things, especially when a friend introduces him. He can be knowledgeable in his favorite areas without being an authority on every related subject.

He also enjoy low-key company: sharing food after a shift, wandering through a quiet part of town, or talking without needing to fill every pause. His hobbies can grow through play and do not need to stay fixed.
