# Upgrade plan 56: the living town (v61.0)

Asked for 2026-11-05: the third of six sets. Townsfolk with routines, crowds, the dog and the pony, chimney smoke,
swinging signs, moths, bats and an owl, and the monsters' idle life were already in the game (plans 19, 24, 25, 45,
46, 51), so this plan is the small life that was missing from Caer Duin.

## v61.0 - small life (69zzk-townlife.js, 26k-washing.js, 76zzzzzzzzzz9-townlife.js, 26-surface.js, 40-render.js)
- Hens in the yards behind the townsfolk's houses: up to three flocks of three to five, wandering their yard and
  pecking, by day and not in rain or snow.
- A cat: on a fence top, a hedge or the well's kerb, a different perch every two hours; by the tavern's door at night,
  its eyes catching the light, and in the wet.
- Pigeons roosting on the ring wall's walk and the roof ridges; they take wing when the party comes within a few
  squares and settle back a while after.
- Washing hung out behind about half the houses: a line from the wall to a post in the yard with three or four cloths
  pegged along it, built into the town's mesh; none in winter.
