# Upgrade plan 22: play beyond the picture (v27.0 - v27.2)

Asked for 2026-11-01: items 46, 47 (the breakables only below ground) and 48 of the list ("9. Gameplay beyond
graphics"). Then plan 23 (sound, 37-40) and plan 24 (monsters, heroes and people, 8-13).

## v27.0 - weather that matters (46)
- Rain, after dark in the Wood, burns a torch twice as fast, and a gust now and then puts it out for a few steps; a
  lantern minds neither. Mist on the surface: two squares of sight, not four, and the view closes in. Snow lying in
  the Wood: a step is two minutes, and the walk is heavier.

## v27.1 - things that break (47, below ground only)
- Crates, barrels and urns stand in the dungeon's rooms: walk into one and it breaks - a few coins, a potion now and
  then, a torch, a reagent, sometimes nothing, sometimes something nasty (rats, a gas). Braziers stand in some halls:
  knocked over (Space), the floor burns for a few turns - a wall of fire between the party and what follows, or a way
  to roast what's in a fight there.

## v27.2 - a dog and a pony (48)
- A dog, bought or found (the farm's litter), walks with the party: it barks at a hidden door or a trap near, digs up
  a buried thing on the land now and then, and growls before an ambush. A pony from the farm carries a share of the
  pack and, on the land's long roads, makes travel quicker - it waits at the shaft's head; it does not go below.
