# Upgrade plan 23: sound (v28.0 - v28.3)

Asked for 2026-11-01: items 37-40 of the list ("7. Sound"). All of it is made by the game itself (Web Audio), as the
rest of its sound is: no recordings.

## v28.0 - sound in its place (37)
- Monsters heard from where they are: to the side they stand on, louder nearer, muffled behind a wall or behind the
  party. Every sound takes the room it is made in: a hall or a cave rings, a corridor is dry, the open air hardly
  answers (a reverb send whose size and share follow the place).

## v28.1 - music that follows (38)
- A layer of tension rises in the depth's tune as monsters come near (a low pulse, a heartbeat), and falls away as
  they are left behind; in safe places - the town by day, a camp, a house's room - the tune drops to its quiet
  self, the pad without the melody's edge.

## v28.2 - the place's own sound (39)
- Birds by day and crickets by night in the Wood, the river near it, the market's murmur in the Square, hammering
  near the forge by day; below, each band its own: water lapping in the Drowned Town and the Flooded Vaults, the
  Foundry's hammers and hiss, bubbling in the Fungal Deep, chimes in the Crystal Caverns, a slow beat at the Root.

## v28.3 - footsteps by the ground (40)
- Cobbles and flagstones, grass and moss, earth, boards (bridges and the houses' rooms), water, and snow lying.
