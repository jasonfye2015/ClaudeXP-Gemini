# Upgrade plan 80: the harbour, the coast, the lighthouse, the boat, and the wider world

Asked for:
- A large, long wooden pier and dock, with a path from it to the town's entrance and a portal from the dock to the
  town's centre.
- Fish in the sea and the river, and a bridge or two over the river.
- Paths to and from the beach, and around the island's outer edge.
- Shells to collect on the beach, coming back every few hours.
- A working lighthouse by the dock.
- A boat to board that opens a sea chart of destinations: other islands, and a mainland continent.

## v130.0: the harbour

- **The land grows** to 101 squares, twelve more rows of sea.
- **The pier:** from the Strand, east of the river's mouth, a ramp climbs off the sand onto a two-wide pier. The pier
  runs eleven squares out over the sea on piles, to a dock twelve squares across.
  - Rails run along the sides, open on the dock's south face where a boat comes alongside, with bollards there.
  - Lanterns on posts along the pier are lit from dusk to dawn.
- **The portal pair:** one portal on the dock and its twin in the town's Square, each taking the party to the other.
- **The Harbour Road:** from the sand north over the falls' stream on a plank bridge, past the falls, through the South
  Meadow and the forest, to the fields' road and on to the West Gate.
- **Places:** the pier, the dock and the road are named. The dock is on the Where-to list and safe at any hour.
- **The water shader:**
  - The sea past the land's edge is deep water: no lilies or bank foam.
  - The sea's squares are water on the renderer's map.
  - The shader's own fish move in it.

## v131.0: the coast and the shore's life

- **Coast paths** around the island's outer edge:
  - The Strand west to the island's west coast, north along it, east along the north coast to the Fortress.
  - Paths down to the beach from the meadows.
  - The edge squares open onto the sea, with no trees beyond the map.
- **Bridges:** the north bridge where the coast path crosses the river, and a bridge at the river's mouth.
- **Fish:** fish to be seen in the sea and the river. Shadows move under the surface, and now and then one jumps and
  splashes.
- **Shells:** shells on the sand to pick up, a few kinds and some rare, coming back every few hours of the game's clock.
  They sell, and Claude is glad of them for his shelf.

## v132.0: the lighthouse

- **The tower:** a tall white-and-red tower on a rock by the dock, its lamp room glazed.
- **Climbing it:** its door opens on a stair to the gallery.
- **The light:**
  - It turns at night, its beam sweeping over the water.
  - It can be seen from the Lookout and the shore.
  - It's lit by a keeper's oil, and the party can tend it.
  - A lit lighthouse is what lets a boat come in at night.

## v133.0: the boat and the sea chart

- **The boat:** it lies at the dock's berth and can be boarded.
- **The sea chart:** boarding opens a chart of the sea, with this island, the other islands and the mainland. Choosing
  one sails there: a crossing with the sea going by, the time it takes, and a fare.
- **To begin with,** the chart shows where the boat can go now.

## v134.0 and on: the destinations

- **Other islands, one release each,** each a surface place of its own. Each has a landing, its own people and beasts,
  something to find, and a way home on the boat.
- **The mainland, a large continent:** a port town first, then the country beyond it, a region at a time.
