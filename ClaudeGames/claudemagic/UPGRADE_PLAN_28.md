# Upgrade plan 28: long-term goals (v33.0 - v33.3)

Asked for 2026-11-02: set 8 of the second list (items 35-38). Achievements, the ascension ladder and Today's dungeon
stand already; these add what the items asked for on top.

## v33.0 - the hall of feats (35)
- A room at the Mage Tower's foot with a plaque for every feat: the achievements as they are, and twenty new ones.

## v33.1 - seasons of the year (36)
- Each season its trial, once a season: spring's flood, summer's long day, autumn's wild hunt, winter's long hunger (the fifteenth floor with nothing eaten) - a
  hard thing to do, and a reward only that season gives.

## v33.2 - New Game+ (37)
- After the Lich King falls, a new run can set out "again": one hero's thing carried as an heirloom, the floors deeper
  and meaner (each time again, more so), and better finds.

## v33.3 - the daily challenge, scored (38)
- Today's dungeon gets set rules besides its mutators (the day's party, the day's kit), a score, and a page of the
  days played with the best kept for each.
