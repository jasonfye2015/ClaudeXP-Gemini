# UPGRADE_PLAN_77 - The wolf den

Asked for: more animals in the forest - a wolf cave, an actual cave and not a door, with a pack living in it: two
large alphas, five slightly smaller adults, four pups at half the size, in various colours; out by day wandering the
forest between the trees (not on the party's paths), all home to sleep at night.

## What it is
- **The den**: west of the river and south of the west glade, a low outcrop of mottled boulders - its own rock, with
  natural rocks along its front, ends and top - and in its face a ragged, unlit cave mouth with bones at the
  threshold. A small trodden clearing before it, walked to from the glade; named "the wolf den", and on the Where-to
  list once seen. Space at the mouth tells what's there by the hour.
- **The pack**: eleven - two alphas (the largest), five grown wolves, four pups at half size - in grey, black, brown,
  tawny and pale coats, as the 3D wolf figure (the painted wolf if the figures aren't loaded).
- **Their day**: out after dawn one at a time; through the day ranging round the den in two groups, each behind its
  alpha, the pups at an adult's heels, stopping to sniff the ground or stand and look; walking freely between the
  trees, never through rock, walls or water; wary of the party, keeping their distance. At dusk they find their way
  home and go in, one after another. At night they're inside - and at the mouth you can hear them breathing.
- Wild, not hostile: the Wood's own wolves by night are another matter.
