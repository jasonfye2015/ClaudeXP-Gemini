# Upgrade plan 32: the dungeon, stranger (v37.0 - v37.4)

Asked for 2026-11-02: set 4 of the second list (items 16-20). Gates with levers and plates, riddle doors, the rival
parties met on a floor, the fallen parties' remains on the same seed, and the wing stairs to the hidden wings stand
already; these add to them.

## v37.0 - floor moods (16)
- Now and then a whole floor has a mood: flooded (water standing in the halls; the torch burns faster), overgrown
  (roots and vines on the walls, things growing to gather), frozen (frost on everything, breath showing; the cold numbs
  the monsters' blows), haunted (a pale fog, wisps; the dead are harder to put down and the heart sinks). Its look, its
  sound, its rule; named when the party comes onto it.

## v37.1 - puzzle rooms (17)
- A room now and then holds a puzzle made fresh for the floor: statues to turn to face the altar, or a beam of light to
  bounce with mirrors to a sun-plate. Solved, a chest opens that only the puzzle opens.

## v37.2 - the rival party (18)
- A named band of rivals goes down ahead of the party through a run: their chalk marks on the walls, the things they
  dropped, their dead - and now and then the band themselves, to trade with, race, or fight.

## v37.3 - lost and found (19)
- The gear a fallen party wore lies where it fell for the next party to recover - on any seed, on that floor, with
  their names on it.

## v37.4 - secret floors (20)
- Now and then a hidden stair (found by searching, or by the hound) leads to a small hand-made vault floor with its own
  story - a drowned chapel, a dwarf's counting-house, a sealed library - its keeper, and its prize.
