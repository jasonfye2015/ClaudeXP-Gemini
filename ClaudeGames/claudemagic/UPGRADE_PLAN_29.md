# Upgrade plan 29: the party's life (v34.0 - v34.4)

Asked for 2026-11-02: set 1 of the second list (items 1-5), retirement offered from level 50. Bonds between heroes
(forty fights together: old friends) and the guildhall's and the house's retirements stand already; these add to them.

## v34.0 - camp scenes (1)
- At a camp the four sit round the fire, painted, and talk about the floors since the last camp - who is hurt, what
  was found, what was slain, a guardian, who fell, the torch, the purse - their lines by who they are.

## v34.1 - bonds and rivals (2)
- Old friends cover each other: a blow at a badly hurt friend is sometimes taken by the other.
- Rivals: two heroes who fight long together and keep score (their kills close) become rivals - each strikes truer to
  outdo the other, and they grumble at the fire.

## v34.2 - scars and stories (3)
- A hero brought to the edge of death and living keeps a scar (three at most): drawn on the portrait, written in the
  sheet with where it came from, and a small trait with it, good or bad.

## v34.3 - letters home (4)
- When the party comes home, every hero standing writes a short letter to the village: where they went, what they
  saw, who they fought beside. All of them kept in the Journal's Letters page.

## v34.4 - retirement to Legends' Row (5)
- A hero of level fifty or more may retire to a house of their own in town, on Legends' Row, and live there; the
  party can visit, and each legend teaches a hero who asks one thing of their calling, once a run.
