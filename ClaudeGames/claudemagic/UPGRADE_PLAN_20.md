# Upgrade plan 20: interface and feel (v25.0 - v25.3)

Asked for 2026-11-01: items 31, 33, 34 and 35 of the list ("6. Interface and feel"); 32 and 36 left for now.

## v25.0 - windows that move (31)
- Windows rise and fade in, and fade out; buttons press in; the purse counts up or down to the new gold with a flash;
  what the party picks up flies from the view to the Inventory button. Still with Reduced motion.

## v25.1 - a living minimap (33)
- Monsters seen on the map (red, the ones in sight; their last place, faded, for those out of it), portals and
  their stones glowing in their colours, the party's trail behind it fading, and zoom (+ / - and the wheel).

## v25.2 - tooltips that compare (34)
- Hovering gear in the pack, a shop or a chest shows it beside what the hero would take it from: each number with
  what goes up (green) and down (red), for the hero it suits best or the one chosen.

## v25.3 - the tray (35)
- Small toasts in a corner tray for what matters and passes: a level gained, a request done, seeds found, a recipe
  or a card learned, a rare find, a renown level - each with its own mark, stacking, fading, and a click opens its page.
