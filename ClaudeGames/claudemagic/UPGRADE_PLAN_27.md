# Upgrade plan 27: making things (v32.0 - v32.3)

Asked for 2026-11-02: set 7 of the second list (items 31-34). Much of it stands already - dishes (69d-cook.js), the
alchemist's recipes and the still (63-camp.js, 69n-fielduse.js), the smith's enchanting, the house and its trophy wall
(67-home.js) - so each phase adds what the item asked for on top of what is there.

## v32.0 - cooking, wider (31)
- Dishes from the South Fields' crops (potato, carrot, tomato, pumpkin, garlic, corn...) and the deep's edible finds
  (salt weed, spore cap, root sap): fifteen new dishes.
- A feast at the camp: up to three different dishes eaten together, their good done together for the next floor.
- A recipe book in the Journal: every dish, known once made, with what it needs.

## v32.1 - enchanting at the Mage Tower (32)
- Relic shards: from the guardians and the bosses, now and then from a champion, and a relic broken down.
- At the Mage Tower's doors the enchanter gives a thing a word it lacks (one of three offered, the player's choice) or
  raises one of its words a tier, for shards and gold; it can fail - the shards spent, and once in a while the thing
  cracked (a word lost).

## v32.2 - brewing at the still (33)
- Recipe pages found on the floors (in chests, on fallen alchemists' remains): each opens a still recipe - crops and a
  band's ingredient into a potion or a tonic, no gold.

## v32.3 - your own house, furnished (34)
- Furnishings bought for the house (a rug, a bookcase, an armour stand...), each a small good for every run.
- The guardians' trophies mounted on the wall (as many as the house's level allows), each giving its guardian's gift.
