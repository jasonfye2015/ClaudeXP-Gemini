# Upgrade plan 33: combat, more to think about (v38.0 - v38.4)

Asked for 2026-11-02: set 5 of the second list (items 21-25). The rows (the back hits for half, polearms and the
Rogue and Ranger reach), the tower shield, the formations, the combos (shatter, knife, kindling, thunderclap), the
bestiary, the guardians' parley and three guardians' second forms stand already; these add to them.

## v38.0 - positioning (21)
- A weapon with the reach word strikes from the back row at full, as a polearm does; a shield turns some blows aimed at
  the hero beside its bearer in the same row; two in the front row who strike the same foe in a round flank it.

## v38.1 - combos (22)
- Four more, from two heroes acting one after the other on the same foe: the feint (a blow, then the Rogue's knife:
  a sure weak spot), the volley (a shot, then a second: half again), smite and sear (holy, then fire: half again),
  hammer and anvil (a Knight's guard or bash, then a blow from beside: the foe's guard broken).

## v38.2 - monster habits (23)
- Every kind has a habit, read once five are slain in a run, and written in the Bestiary for good: who it goes for, when it runs, what
  it does first. Read after five of a kind slain in one expedition: told at the fight's start, and a tenth more against that kind; kept in the Bestiary.

## v38.3 - surrender and parley (24)
- Foes that can speak, badly beaten, now and then throw down their weapons: spare them (they go, leaving their purse,
  and the heart lifts) or finish them. A kind spared often enough remembers: one of them may turn up later with a word
  of warning, or a map.

## v38.4 - boss phases (25)
- Every guardian and the Lich King changes halfway: a new form and tactic of its own, the music turning, a card with
  its words.
