# UPGRADE_PLAN_78 - The south of the Wood, and the deer

Asked for: the forest expanded, for more room for animals, and a herd of deer roaming it.

## What it is
- **The Wood grows south**: from 61 squares to 81 - twenty rows of old forest more, the river running on through
  them. The east beyond the cliffs is the hill (the cliffs, the gate and the town side exactly where they were).
  Everything the seed placed in the old land is where it was, square for square: the new land is laid afterwards by
  dice of its own (and the seed's picks are bounded to the old land), so no hut, cave, shrine, node or glade moves.
- **Two meadows**: the South Meadow east of the river, reached by a track south from Claude's clearing; the Long
  Meadow west of it, over a second, shallow ford (stepping stones and all), with a deer track north through the trees
  to the wolf den.
- **Old saves**: the map a party had seen in the smaller Wood is read at the size it was saved at.
- **The herd**: ten - a stag, six does in a few shades, three fawns about half their size - grazing the South Meadow by
  day, heads down, now and then one standing to look; bedding at dusk among the trees at the meadow's edge, back out
  after dawn. They move as the wolves do, between the trees. If the party comes near, or one of the den's wolves,
  the whole herd bolts at a gallop and drifts back when it's quiet.
