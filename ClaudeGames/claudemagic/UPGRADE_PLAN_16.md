# Upgrade plan 16: the Portal Court (v21.0 - v21.3)

Asked for 2026-11-01, all of it. The Keep-yard had grown to eight portals packed round the shaft in a 5x5 yard, the
Wood's portals the same arch in different tints, nothing saying where each goes or what waits there.

## v21.0 - the Portal Court
- The yard's walls pushed out to [32,15]-[40,25] (inside 7x9, from 5x5), still open to Shaft Lane on the west.
- The shaft in the middle; the Wood's portals on the north and south walls (the Mage Tower, Leshy's cabin,
  Claudeita's cave / the Fortress, the Glyph Deep), the world gates on the east wall (Haven's red portal, the Neather
  Gate) - a walk from the opening to every one of them, the middle rows clear. The postern moves to the new corner.
- A name stone at each portal's foot and a brazier either side in its own colour; a ring of runes on the floor before
  it.

## v21.1 - the Wayfarer's Stone ("the Waystone"; named apart from the dungeon camps' waystones)
- A waystone on a dais by the opening: it takes the party to any place in the Wood they've already walked to - the
  Wood gate, the First Glade, the farm, the Mage Tower's clearing, Leshy's cabin, Claudeita's crag, the Fortress, the
  Glyph Deep's cabinet - and its twin by the Wood's road takes them back to the court. New places join by being found.

## v21.2 - the portals' own looks
- Each portal its own frame and surface: the Tower's rune-cut violet stone, the cabin's roots and antlers, the cave's
  rough rock and sand, the Fortress's iron-banded black stone, Haven's red gothic arch, the Glyph Deep's green letters
  (the Neather Gate keeps its basalt). A glimpse of the far side in each, by day or night. The twins in the Wood the same.
- Facing one: its name and what's waiting there on the hint line. A badge on each - one figure for one hero, four for
  the party - and who may go (fighters, casters).
- Crossing: a swirl and a fade, and each portal its own note.

## v21.3 - the court's keepers
- The noticeboard: every place beyond the portals, its next step and what's waiting.
- The Portal-warden, who keeps the court: who may go where, what each place is, and a hint of what to do next.
- The lift: the shaft's winch lowers the party to any merchant's camp they've reached, or to the first floor.
