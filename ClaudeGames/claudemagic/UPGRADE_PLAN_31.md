# Upgrade plan 31: the town alive (v36.0 - v36.4)

Asked for 2026-11-02: set 3 of the second list (items 11-15). The town's festivals (24b-calendar.js), the week's
peddler (23-town.js), the residents' routines (76zzzzy-townsfolk.js) and the court's noticeboard stand already.

## v36.0 - market days (11)
- Traders from away, each on a fixed day of the run's week: the dwarf armourer, the spice-wife from the east, the old
  tinker - and on the market day itself all of them and a bookseller, their stalls round the Square, selling what the
  town's own shops never have.

## v36.1 - festivals, kept (12)
- A festival's week dresses the town (bunting and lanterns about the Square), plays its own tune coming into town, and
  holds three games for the party - a test of arm, of eye and of wit - once each a festival, and a prize for winning all
  three: a favour of that festival, once a year.

## v36.2 - townsfolk who remember (13)
- Walking past the residents, they greet the party by what it has done - the deepest floor, a guardian, a fall, a feat -
  and now and then pass on a lead: a den the hound could find, the trader due, the season's trial, a page of the still's.

## v36.3 - building the town (14)
- Projects the village raises with gold and the island's timber and stone: the old wall repaired, a stone bridge for
  the plank one, a bathhouse, a watchtower - each changing the town's map, and each a small good.

## v36.4 - the news board (15)
- The Caer Duin Crier, a sheet a week on the noticeboard, written from the runs: the fallen, the records, the fields'
  prizes, the casino's big winners, the new legends and feats.
