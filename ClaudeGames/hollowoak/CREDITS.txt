The Hollow Oak - what it's made of, and where it came from.

The textures (assets/textures): ambientCG (CC0), https://ambientcg.com - Bark007 (the heartwood), Ground103 (the earth),
ScatteredLeaves001 (the dry leaves); colour and normal maps, resized to 512, tinted in the game.

Claude's wand (assets/wand.npz, wand.png): Kay Lousberg's KayKit Adventurers pack (CC0), https://kaylousberg.com -
its shape and colours copied out once by tools/export_wand.py.

To come (phase 3): the spider, Ysmeine and her small ones - Quaternius's animated Spider (CC0), https://quaternius.com.

The code, the tree, its light and its words: written for this game. The small OpenGL layer (engine/gl.py) is the one the
Timeline's other first-person games use, copied here so the game stands on its own.
