The Hollow Oak
==============

One night. One host. Get to dawn.

A short, deadly serious first-person romance: one night as the guest of Ysmeine, the Weaver Below - an ancient spider
who lives inside the Hollow Oak - played from dusk to dawn. The absurd premise is never winked at; the gravity of the
telling is what makes it unsettling. One sitting, two to four hours. It stands alone: it shares nothing with the
Timeline's other games.

The design: "The Hollow Oak - Design Document" (Claude Docs), https://claude.ai/artifact/JtQZ3J468Lqh9E8QqoVXfS

Playing (phases 1 and 2: the tree, and her silk)
  Click to take the mouse (Esc gives it back, and pauses). W A S D walk - slowly; Shift runs; Space jumps.
  E (or L) shakes the lantern, hung from the crook of his wand. It's old and low on oil: it flickers, it sputters, and now and then it gutters out -
  a shake usually brings it back. Usually.

  In through the roots, into the hollow: the inside of the tree going up and up into the dark, a ledge winding up its
  wall past chambers opening into the wood - the gallery, the larder, the high hollow - to the crown, broken open to the
  sky - a deep night, its stars twinkling, a faint aurora wavering across it. Glowing fungus in pockets, each breathing slowly in and out, moonlight in narrow shafts through the knot-holes, and fog: low layers of it curling
  over the floor of the hollow, and wisps of mist hanging in the air, catching the lantern's light. And high
  in the dark, something watching you: eight red lights, across the hollow and above. She keeps to the dark.

  Her silk: three strands thrown across the hollow from one turn of the ledge to a higher one - a shorter way up, and a
  test of nerve - her loom, a great web across the hollow low down, and a web across the crown under the moon. On a
  strand you sway, more the faster you go; A and D lean against it. Lean too far and you're off. Every step on silk
  hums through the whole web, and she feels it: careful steps, or hurried ones, slips and falls. Her small ones are
  everywhere - on the wall, among the roots, out on the silk - and gone in a scurry when you come near. And now and
  then one runs right across your eyes (two at once, and he swipes them off), or drops on a thread to hang before your face. And when the lantern's out,
  they come up the wand for it.

  How you hold up shows in two orbs, each with two fluids: red health with green poison rising under it (bottom left),
  blue sanity with yellow stupor rising under it (bottom right). If either reaches the other - she has you. The edge
  of the view beats when it comes close. Hard falls hurt, bites poison, the dark wears at your mind. Between the
  orbs, a monitor shows your heart.

  Before the night (after the title): begin, the endings you've found, your records, the options - among them what
  writes her words (a language model on this computer, or a script) and whether you hear her voice aloud.

  Sound: the wind in the trunk, the silk humming and creaking under you, small legs skittering away, the lantern's
  rattle. It follows the computer's mute.

Running it
  In the Timeline: the launcher's Apps, or Games on any of the computers.
  On its own: python tools/play.py (with the Timeline's Python).
  The tests: python tests/run.py

Built in Python, PySide6 and OpenGL 3.3 (PyOpenGL). The night (game/) is plain Python; the tests walk it.
