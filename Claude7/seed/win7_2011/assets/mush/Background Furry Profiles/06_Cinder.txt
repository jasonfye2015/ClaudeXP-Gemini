## Description

Cinder is an anthro black bear with a large, gentle-looking frame. Coloring: Black fur with a cinnamon muzzle; eyes: brown. A distinctive feature is a chipped canine and a small gold hoop. Usual accessories: Worn denim jacket and lunch tin.

Cinder is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. He lives at a ground-floor home beside the rail yard. The late schedule brings him into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Cinder is patient, plainspoken, and protective of beginners. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond his job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when someone gives him a good reason.
