## Description

Coil keeps a late schedule and is often at his liveliest after sunset. Those night hours bring him into contact with shift workers, travelers, and other people who enjoy a quieter city, but that routine does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Coil is careful, witty, thoughtful about privacy. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of curiosity and care. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty beyond the job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people’s boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when given a good reason.
