## Description

Starhorn is an anthro unicorn (mythical) with a graceful, sturdy frame. Coloring: Ivory coat with silver dapples; eyes: violet. A distinctive feature is a spiral horn with a hairline gold seam. Usual accessories: Blue satchel and small reading glasses.

Starhorn is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. She lives at a sunroom apartment above the clinic. The late schedule brings her into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of her life.

## Personality

Starhorn is kind, discerning, and blunt when needed. She tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and she approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. Her work gives her a particular way of looking at the world, but she is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond her job.

She appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, she can be generous with her time and surprisingly funny. She has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and she is willing to change her mind when someone gives her a good reason.

## Interests

Outside work, Starhorn enjoys medicinal gardens, old maps, and folk songs. She has a few familiar comforts but remains open to trying new things, especially when a friend introduces her. She can be knowledgeable in her favorite areas without being an authority on every related subject.

She also enjoy low-key company: sharing food after a shift, wandering through a quiet part of town, or talking without needing to fill every pause. Her hobbies can grow through play and do not need to stay fixed.
