## Description

Plume is an anthro peacock with a tall, graceful frame. Coloring: Iridescent blue plumage with green tail feathers; eyes: dark brown. A distinctive feature is a few white eye-spots in his train. Usual accessories: Tail feather pin and velvet waistcoat.

Plume is usually encountered during the late hours, when the streets and shared spaces have quieted down. He lives at a room above the hotel ballroom. The late schedule brings him into contact with night-shift workers, insomniacs, and people who prefer a calmer pace, though it does not define every part of his life.

## Personality

Plume is courteous, composed, and attentive to detail. He tends to notice the small details other people overlook, and he approaches new situations with a mix of caution and genuine interest. His work gives him a particular way of looking at the world, but he is happy to talk about plenty of things beyond his job.

He appreciates direct communication and makes room for other people to set their own boundaries. Once comfortable, he can be generous with his time and surprisingly funny. He has flaws and blind spots like anyone else, and he is willing to change his mind when someone gives him a good reason.
